# M-209 转轮密码机（M-209 (Hagelin C-38)）

> 机器图鉴 · 1942–1960s · 美国 / 1942–1960s · USA · 关联游戏：`m209`

## 概述

**中文**：六轮凸轮齿条转轮机：美军的「便携恩尼格玛」，比 Enigma 小巧得多。

**English**: Six cam-and-pin wheels — America's "pocket Enigma", far more portable than its German counterpart.

## 历史

**中文**：瑞典工程师哈格林的设计，1942 年美军采用，生产 14 万台，从诺曼底用到越战初期。它的密钥周期约 26 位 × 26 × 26 × 26 × 25 × 23，但实际周期远小于理论值——这为后来的已知明文攻击埋下伏笔。

**English**: Designed by Swedish engineer Boris Hagelin and adopted by the US in 1942, 140,000 units served from Normandy into the early Vietnam era. Its nominal period is huge, but the real cycle is far shorter — a weakness later exploited by known-plaintext attacks.
