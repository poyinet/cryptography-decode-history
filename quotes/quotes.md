### **奥古斯特·克尔克霍夫斯**（Auguste Kerckhoffs） · 1883

**中文**：密码体制不应当要求保密，即使落入敌人手中也不应带来不便。

**English**: The system must not require secrecy, and it must be able to fall into the hands of the enemy without inconvenience.

### **奥古斯特·克尔克霍夫斯**（Auguste Kerckhoffs） · 1883

**中文**：密码体制即便在数学上无法保证，也必须在实际上无法破译。

**English**: The system must be practically, if not mathematically, indecipherable.

### **克劳德·香农**（Claude Shannon） · 1949

**English**: Cryptography is about communication in the presence of adversaries.

**English**: There are two kinds of cryptography in this world: cryptography that will stop your kid sister from reading your files, and cryptography that will stop major governments from reading your files.

### **克劳德·香农**（Claude Shannon） · 1949

**中文**：完美保密性定义为：要求系统在密文被敌人截获之后，各种可能消息的后验概率与截获之前这些消息的先验概率完全相同。

**English**: Perfect secrecy is defined by requiring of a system that after a cryptogram is intercepted by the enemy the a posteriori probabilities of the various possible messages be exactly the same as the a priori probabilities of the same messages before the interception.
