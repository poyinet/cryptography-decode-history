# 古代起源（Ancient Origins）

> 前 500 BCE 前 · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：在「密码」这个词出现之前，人类已经偷偷通信了很久。最早的密写不是写字，而是「记」：中国结绳记事把结的位置当信息，苏美尔人在楔形文字泥板上故意写错来藏真意，古埃及人把象形符号画进只有祭司读得懂的暗记。斯巴达人用「密码棒」（scytale）——把一条皮条卷在圆棒上写字，只有持同样圆棒的人展开才能读。这一代还没有「加密算法」的概念，秘密靠的是**载体本身**：没有那根棒、那本泥板，密文就是一团乱。它的思想内核延续至今——「密钥就在那根棒上」，只是今天的棒变成了数字参数。

**English**: Long before the word "cipher" existed, humans had been keeping secrets for a long time. The earliest ciphers were not writing but "recording": Chinese knotted cords encoded information in the position of knots, Sumerians deliberately wrote errors on cuneiform tablets to hide the true meaning, and ancient Egyptians painted hieroglyphs only priests could read. The Spartans used the scytale — a leather strip wound around a rod, letters written, only the matching rod unrolls it legibly. This generation had no concept of an "encryption algorithm"; secrecy rested on the **carrier itself** — without that rod or tablet, the ciphertext is a scramble. Its core idea survives to today: "the key is on that rod" — only now the rod is a number.
