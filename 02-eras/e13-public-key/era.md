# 公钥革命（Public-key Revolution）

> 1976–1990 · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：公钥密码的诞生，彻底解决了「密钥怎么给」这个困扰密码学千年的难题。1976 年 Diffie 和 Hellman 提出密钥交换：两人不必事先共享密钥，却能协商出一个共享秘密，而窃听者算不出来——这是公钥的开端。次年 RSA（Rivest-Shamir-Adleman）把公钥加密变成可计算的算法，基于「大数分解难」。同一时期，默克尔（Merkle）在公钥签名、可证明安全上独立推进。公钥的意义不只是「加密」，而是**密钥可以公开**——这催生了数字签名、PKI、整个互联网的信任体系。1976 到 1990 这十四年，是密码学史上思想密度最高的时期。

**English**: The birth of public-key cryptography finally solved "how to deliver the key," the problem that had plagued cryptography for a thousand years. In 1976 Diffie and Hellman proposed key exchange: two parties need not share a key in advance yet can agree on a shared secret that an eavesdropper cannot compute — the start of public-key. The following year RSA (Rivest-Shamir-Adleman) turned public-key encryption into a computable algorithm, based on "integer factorization is hard." In the same period, Merkle independently pushed forward public-key signatures and provable security. The meaning of public-key is not just "encryption" but **keys can be public** — this spawned digital signatures, PKI, and the entire trust system of the internet. 1976–1990 is the most ideationally dense period in cryptography’s history.
