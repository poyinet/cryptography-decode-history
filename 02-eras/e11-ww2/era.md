# 第二次世界大战（World War II）

> 1939–1945 · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：二战是密码学第一次「人类 vs 机器」的大对决。德军把恩尼格玛用到极致（4 转子 + 插线板，组合数高达天文数字），英军图灵的 Bombe 和波兰三杰（雷耶夫斯基、罗życki、齐加利斯克）用数学把不可能穷举的转子设置筛了出来；Colossus 是历史上第一台「为了破译而造的电子计算机」，专门破洛伦兹。太平洋战场，美军破译日本紫密（Rowlett 带队）和 JN-25（中途岛海战的关键），纳瓦霍语密码通话员全程没被日军破开。这一代确立了「密码战决定战争胜负」，战后布莱切利园的保密成果直到 1970 年代才解密——人类史上最大规模的保密，反过来喂养了密码学的进步。

**English**: WWII was the first "humans vs. machines" duel in cryptography. Germany pushed Enigma to its limit (4 rotors + plugboard, combinations of astronomical size); Turing’s Bombe and the Polish trio (Rejewski, Różycki, Zygalski) used mathematics to sieve rotor settings that brute force could never reach. Colossus — the first electronic computer built *to break a cipher* — was dedicated to the Lorenz. In the Pacific, the Americans broke Japan’s Purple (Rowlett’s team) and JN-25 (the key to the Battle of Midway), while the Navajo Code Talkers’ language was never broken by the Japanese. This generation established "codebreaking decides wars," and Bletchley Park’s classified results stayed secret until the 1970s — the largest secrecy in human history, which in turn fed cryptography’s advance.
