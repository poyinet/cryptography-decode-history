# 文艺复兴与宗教改革（Renaissance & Reformation）

> 1450–1600 · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：多表替换的发明，让密码学第一次「赢」了频率分析。阿尔贝蒂 1467 年设计的密码盘（一个可旋转的字母环）是同代人最早的机械多表；维吉尼亚 1553 年的维吉尼亚表则系统化地用「密钥逐字母轮换」——同一个明文字母在不同位置变成不同密文字母，字母频率被彻底抹平。从此单表频率分析失效，维吉尼亚密码号称「不可解」（la chiffre indéchiffrable）。这一代还有苏格兰玛丽女王的悲剧：她一生用替换密码写信，1586 年她写给合谋者的密信被巴比宁用「数重复字母间隔」猜破，成为她死刑的证据。隐写与外交密码在这一代成熟，培根的双字体、书密码都出自这个时期。

**English**: The invention of polyalphabetic substitution was the first time cryptography *beat* frequency analysis. Alberti’s 1467 cipher disk (a rotating letter ring) was the earliest mechanical multi-table of its day; Vigenère’s 1553 table systematized "the key rotates letter by letter" — the same plaintext letter becomes a different cipher letter at different positions, so letter frequencies are flattened completely. Monoalphabetic frequency analysis died here, and the Vigenère cipher was called "the indecipherable cipher." This generation also holds the tragedy of Mary Queen of Scots: she wrote her whole life in substitution, and in 1586 a letter she sent to a co-conspirator was cracked by Babington via "counting repeated-letter intervals" — the evidence for her execution. Steganography and diplomatic ciphers matured here; Bacon’s biliteral scheme and book ciphers all come from this period.
