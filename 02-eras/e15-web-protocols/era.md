# 网络与协议时代（Web & Protocol Era）

> 1991–2010 · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：算法成熟后，战场移到「协议」——怎么把加密拼成真正可用的安全通信。TLS（传输层安全，HTTPS 的底层）用 Diffie-Hellman 做密钥交换、用 AES 加密、用数字签名防篡改，一个往返完成握手；PGP 和 S/MIME 让电子邮件加密走进普通用户；X.509 证书把「这个公钥属于这个人」的信任交给证书颁发机构（CA）的链条，Let’s Encrypt 后来让免费证书成为常态。这一代的敌人不再是「破算法」，而是「实现与协议漏洞」——心跳攻击、降级攻击、中间人。密码学第一次意识到：算法再强，协议有洞就全白搭。

**English**: Once algorithms matured, the battlefield moved to "protocols" — how to assemble encryption into genuinely usable secure communication. TLS (Transport Layer Security, the base of HTTPS) uses Diffie-Hellman for key exchange, AES for encryption, digital signatures against tampering, completing the handshake in one round trip; PGP and S/MIME brought email encryption to ordinary users; X.509 certificates delegated "this public key belongs to this person" to a chain of certificate authorities (CAs), and Let’s Encrypt later made free certificates routine. This generation’s enemy was no longer "breaking the algorithm" but "implementation and protocol flaws" — heartbleed, downgrade attacks, man-in-the-middle. Cryptography first realized: however strong the algorithm, a hole in the protocol wastes it all.
