# 古典希腊罗马（Classical Greece & Rome）

> 前 500–476 CE · 轴1 编年叙事

**中文**：这一代第一次把密写变成**可操作的规则**。斯巴达的 scytale 之外，还有 Laconic 暗号（约定某些词代表特定意思）；凯撒把整个字母表向后移 3 位——史上第一个「移位密码」，他本人用它加密军令，还留下「Veni, vidi, vici」的名句。犹太人发明的 Atbash（把 a 变 z、b 变 y，字母表对折）是替换思想的另一种形态。波利比奥斯设计了 5×5 的格子，把字母变成坐标——这个「坐标替字母」的思路，两千年后还在格密码里活着。这一代的核心是**替换与换位的手艺**，破译靠的是猜和字典，还没人意识到「频率」里藏着破绽。

**English**: This generation was the first to turn secrecy into **operable rules**. Beyond the Spartan scytale there was the Laconic (agreed words standing for specific meanings); Caesar shifted the whole alphabet back by three — the first "shift cipher" in history, which he used for military orders, and which left the famous "Veni, vidi, vici." The Atbash invented by the Jews (a→z, b→y, folding the alphabet in half) was another form of the substitution idea. Polybius designed a 5×5 grid, turning letters into coordinates — the idea of "coordinates standing for letters" still lives in grid ciphers two millennia later. The core of this generation was the **craft of substitution and transposition**; breaking it relied on guesswork and dictionaries, and no one yet realized the flaw hid in "frequency."
