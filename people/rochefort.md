# 罗奇福特（Rochefort）

> 人物志 · ⚓ · 美国海军密码破译专家 · 珍珠港 HYPO 站站长 · 1900-1976 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：约瑟夫·罗奇福特生于纽约，当过水兵，会说日语，也懂无线电。他主持珍珠港 HYPO 站，1942 年春把 JN-25 的破译推到实战可用；他设计的「AF 淡水」陷阱让中途岛从疑团变成坐标，为尼米兹赢得设伏的时间。战后他一度被调离情报岗位，荣誉迟到数十年——1990 年代，他的名字才被写进美国海军的荣誉殿堂。

**English**: Joseph Rochefort was born in New York, served as a sailor, learned Japanese, and understood radio. Running Station HYPO at Pearl Harbor, he pushed the JN-25 break into operational use by spring 1942; his "AF water" trap turned Midway from a riddle into coordinates, buying Nimitz time to set his ambush. reassigned away from intelligence after the war, honours came decades late — only in the 1990s was his name written into the Navy's hall of honour.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：罗奇福特会说日语，年轻时在日本待过；他设计的「AF 淡水陷阱」让日军自己供出了中途岛——破译史上最优雅的钓鱼。

**English**: Fun fact: Rochefort learned Japanese in his youth; his "AF fresh water" trap made the Japanese confess Midway — the most elegant fishing expedition in codebreaking history.

## 名言

**中文**：「只要没人计较功劳归谁，我们什么都能做到。」

**English**: "We can do anything provided no one cares who gets the credit."
