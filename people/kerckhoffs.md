# 奥古斯特·克尔克霍夫斯（Auguste Kerckhoffs）

> 人物志 · 📏 · Kerckhoffs 原则的提出者 · 现代密码设计的第一准则 · 1835–1903 · 荷兰

## 简介

**中文**：奥古斯特·克尔克霍夫斯是荷兰语言学家与密码学家，大半生在法国教书。1883 年，他在法国《军事科学杂志》发表《军事密码学》，提出著名的「克尔克霍夫斯原则」：密码体制的安全性应完全依赖密钥保密，即便算法被对手彻底掌握也不应被攻破。这一原则日后成为现代密码设计的铁律，也是「算法公开、密钥保密」这一当代加密体系的思想源头。

**English**: Auguste Kerckhoffs was a Dutch linguist and cryptographer who spent most of his career teaching in Paris. In 1883 he published "La cryptographie militaire," setting out six design desiderata for military ciphers. The second became famous as Kerckhoffs' principle: a cryptosystem must remain secure even if everything about it except the key falls into enemy hands. Modern cryptography treats this as a foundational rule — the reason today's standard algorithms such as AES are public, while only the keys stay secret.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：克尔克霍夫斯的本职是语言学家，晚年积极推广人造语言「沃拉普克语」，还担任其官方学院的主持人；密码学只是他的副业，却留下了比主业更不朽的遗产。

**English**: Fun fact: Kerckhoffs' day job was linguistics — he was a leading champion of Volapük, a constructed international language, and directed its academy. Cryptography was a side project, yet it is the side project that made his name immortal.

## 名言

**中文**：「体制不应要求保密，落入敌手也不应造成麻烦。」

**English**: "The system must not require secrecy; it must be able to fall into enemy hands without inconvenience."
