# 奥德·雷格夫（Oded Regev）

> 人物志 · 🧮 · 计算机科学家 · LWE 问题奠基者 · 1979– · 以色列

## 简介

**中文**：以色列密码学家，后量子加密的奠基人之一。2005 年他提出基于「格上最短向量问题」（LWE）的公钥加密——这个难题在量子计算机面前依然困难，于是 LWE 成了后量子密码的数学地基。他后来又提出基于「环上带误差」（RLWE）的更实用版本，直接催生了 Kyber/ML-KEM。Regev 的工作是「换掉公钥密码的数学根基」这一整条路线的起点，没有他，NIST 2024 的后量子标准就没有根基。

**English**: An Israeli cryptographer, one of the founders of post-quantum encryption. In 2005 he proposed public-key encryption based on the "shortest vector in a lattice" (LWE) problem — hard even for a quantum computer, so LWE became the mathematical foundation of post-quantum crypto. He later gave a more practical version based on "ring with errors" (RLWE), directly spawning Kyber/ML-KEM. Regev's work is the starting point of the whole "replace public-key's math foundation" line; without him, NIST's 2024 post-quantum standards would have no foundation.

## 一句话

**中文**：LWE 的「带误差」是它的灵魂：密钥加一个微小噪声，破解者无法分离信号与噪声。

**English**: The "error" in LWE is its soul: the key plus a tiny noise — the attacker cannot separate signal from noise.
