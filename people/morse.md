# 塞缪尔·摩斯（Samuel Morse）

> 人物志 · 📡 · 发明家 · 摩斯电码之父 · 1791–1872 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：电报的发明者，也是「用代码代替语言」这个密码思想的早期实践者。他和助手 Vail 设计的莫尔斯电码，把字母拆成点划组合——严格说是「编码」（code）而非「加密」（cipher），因为它没有密钥、任何人都能查表读懂。但正是电报让「保密通信」第一次成为国家刚需：战场、外交、金融的电报都要保密，于是电报加密（从一次性密钥到后来的各种电报密码）这门学问才真正被催熟。没有摩斯，就没有后来那一大串电报密码战史。

**English**: The inventor of the telegraph, and an early practitioner of the "code instead of language" idea. The Morse code he and Vail designed broke letters into dot-dash patterns — strictly a "code", not a "cipher", since it has no key and anyone can read it from a table. But the telegraph made "secure communication" a national must-have for the first time: battlefield, diplomatic, and financial traffic all needed secrecy, and that is what ripened the craft of telegraph cryptography (from one-time keys to the later telegraph ciphers). Without Morse, there would be no great history of telegraph codebreaking after him.

## 一句话

**中文**：第一封电报「What hath God wrought」是 1844 年在华盛顿—巴尔的摩间发出的，他本人亲手发的点划。

**English**: The very first telegram, "What hath God wrought", was sent in 1844 between Washington and Baltimore — the dots and dashes he sent himself.
