# 焦万·巴蒂斯塔·贝拉索（Giovan Battista Bellaso）

> 人物志 · 🔑 · 多表替换密码的真正发明者 · 维吉尼亚密码的原作者 · 约 1505–? · 意大利

## 简介

**中文**：焦万·巴蒂斯塔·贝拉索是意大利密码学家。1553 年，他发表《焦万·巴蒂斯塔·贝拉索先生的密码》，首次系统描述多表替换密码：以密钥词轮换字母表（即「塔布拉方阵」，拉丁文 tabula recta），使同一明文字母在不同位置呈现不同密文，令频率分析失效。三十三年后维吉尼亚发表的方案核心正是贝拉索的设计——这套体制被后世冠以维吉尼亚之名，实为张冠李戴。

**English**: Giovan Battista Bellaso was an Italian cryptologist from Brescia. In 1553 he published a treatise on ciphers that described, for the first time in print, polyalphabetic substitution: a keyword rotates through a series of alphabets — the tabula recta — so the same plaintext letter is enciphered differently at every position, defeating simple frequency analysis. Thirty-three years later Blaise de Vigenère published a cipher built squarely on Bellaso's core idea, and history attached Vigenère's name to it. The "Vigenère cipher" is, in truth, Bellaso's cipher.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：贝拉索 1553 年的小册子还首创「互反字母表」——明文表与密文表互为倒序，加密与解密变成同一操作；这一便捷设计被后世众多密码沿用，而贝拉索本人的生卒年月却几乎失考。

**English**: Fun fact: Bellaso's 1553 booklet also introduced reciprocal alphabets — plaintext and ciphertext alphabets reversed against each other so enciphering and deciphering become the same operation. Yet almost nothing else about his life is known, not even his year of death.

## 名言

**中文**：密钥词所至，字母表随之轮转——这便是多表密码的全部秘密。

**English**: Wherever the keyword goes, the alphabet turns with it — that is the whole secret of polyalphabetic ciphers.
