# 布莱斯·德·维吉尼亚（Blaise de Vigenère）

> 人物志 · ✒️ · 多表替换密码的集大成者 · 后世以其命名此体系 · 1523–1596 · 法国

## 简介

**中文**：布莱斯·德·维吉尼亚是法国外交官与密码学家。他出身外交世家，青年时出使罗马，在教廷钻研大量密码文献。1586 年，他出版《密码论》，系统整理并推广多表替换思想，还独创了「自动密钥」密码——让明文字母自身延伸为密钥。虽然这套方案的核心思想源自意大利人贝拉索，19 世纪起它却被冠以维吉尼亚之名，成为西方密码史上最著名的「不可破译的密码」，直到 1863 年才被卡西斯基攻破。

**English**: Blaise de Vigenère was a French diplomat and cryptographer. Born into a diplomatic family, he served on missions to Rome, where he studied the cipher literature of the papal court. In 1586 he published Traicté des chiffres, a comprehensive treatise that popularised polyalphabetic substitution and introduced his own invention, the autokey cipher, in which the plaintext itself extends the key. Though the core scheme had actually been devised by the Italian Bellaso decades earlier, 19th-century writers attached Vigenère's name to it — and the "Vigenère cipher" became the most celebrated "indecipherable cipher" of the Western world until Kasiski broke it in 1863.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：维吉尼亚晚年痴迷炼金术与神秘学，《密码论》出版时他已年过六旬——密码学只是这位外交官多彩人生的晚年一章，却成了他身后最响亮的名字。

**English**: Fun fact: Vigenère wrote his famous treatise in his sixties, after a long life as diplomat and alchemist — cryptography was a late hobby, yet it made his name immortal.
