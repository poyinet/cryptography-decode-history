# 伊丽莎白·弗里德曼（Elizebeth Friedman）

> 人物志 · 🌹 · 「美国密码学第一夫人」· 走私犯与纳粹间谍的克星 · 1892–1980 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：伊丽莎白·弗里德曼是美国密码学家，威廉·弗里德曼之妻，人称「美国密码学第一夫人」。一战时她成为美国首批女性密码破译员，率先把统计学方法系统引入密码分析。1930 年代禁酒令时期，她为美国海岸警卫队与财政部破译数千份走私犯密电，把贩酒集团头目一一送上法庭；二战中她识破盘踞南美的纳粹间谍网，还破获了著名的「娃娃女人」间谍案。晚年她与丈夫合著《莎士比亚密码》，用统计方法检验莎剧署名之谜。

**English**: Elizebeth Friedman was an American cryptanalyst — wife of William Friedman — and is remembered as the "first lady of American cryptology." During the First World War she became one of America's first female codebreakers and an early champion of statistical methods. In the Prohibition era she broke thousands of messages for the Coast Guard and Treasury, sending rum-running gang leaders to prison; in the Second World War she unmasked Nazi spy rings in South America, including the notorious "Doll Woman" case. In retirement she and her husband wrote The Shakespearean Ciphers Examined, applying statistics to claims of hidden messages in the plays.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：她的名字 Elizebeth 刻意少用一个字母（拼作 -ze- 而非 -za-），只为与常见的 Elizabeth 区分——可直到今天，她的墓碑与纪念文字仍时常被人拼错。

**English**: Fun fact: her name "Elizebeth" was deliberately spelled with an unusual -ze- to set it apart from the common Elizabeth — yet even her tombstone and modern tributes still get it wrong.
