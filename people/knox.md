# 迪利·诺克斯（Alfred Dillwyn "Dilly" Knox）

> 人物志 · 🎓 · 布莱切利园最早的破译者 · 「迪利的姑娘们」的导师 · 1884–1943 · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：迪利·诺克斯是英国古典学学者与密码学家。一战时他效力于海军部40号房间，破译德国海军密码。1937 年，他用自创的「钓竿」(rodding) 法——以金属杆模拟转子接线——破解了意大利海军 Enigma，为 1941 年马塔潘角海战的关键情报奠定基础。1941 年 12 月，他带领「迪利的姑娘们」首次破译德方军事情报机构 Abwehr 的 Enigma，让盟军得以监视整个德国间谍网。他 1943 年因癌症去世，功绩多年后才为世人所知。

**English**: Dilly Knox was an English classical scholar turned cryptanalyst, and one of Bletchley Park's first codebreakers. Recruited into Room 40 during the First World War, he cut his teeth breaking German naval codes. In 1937 he cracked the Italian Navy's Enigma, devising his celebrated "rodding" method — metal rods that simulated the rotor wirings — an achievement that underpinned vital intelligence for the Battle of Cape Matapan in 1941. His greatest triumph came in December 1941, when he and his "Dilly's girls" (Mavis Lever and Margaret Rock) broke the Abwehr's Enigma, exposing the entire German military-intelligence spy network. Knox died of cancer in 1943; his work stayed classified for decades.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：破译 Abwehr Enigma 的关键线索，是德方密文几乎都以「ATTENTION」开头——玛维斯·利弗发现这个固定格式后，等于把一段明文直接送进了迪利团队的破译流程。

**English**: Fun fact: the Abwehr Enigma fell partly because German messages so often began "ATTENTION" — Mavis Lever spotted the giveaway, handing Dilly's team a chunk of free plaintext.
