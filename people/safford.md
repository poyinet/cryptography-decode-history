# 劳伦斯·萨福德（Laurance Safford）

> 人物志 · 🌊 · 美国海军密码局长 · OP-20-G 主官 · 1893–1973 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：美国陆军密码分析的先驱。一战时他接手美国版的「40 号房」，专门破译德国海军密码——包括那个著名的 U-110 潜艇密码（Narrative Code）。他靠的是对德军密码习惯的耐心分析而非运气。一战后他把破译体系制度化，是美国现代密码分析的奠基人之一。那个年代，美国破译能力从零起步，萨福德就是起步的那批人。

**English**: A pioneer of US Army cryptanalysis. In WWI he took over the American "Room 40", breaking German naval codes — including the famous U-110 submarine Narrative Code. He relied on patient analysis of German cipher habits, not luck. After the war he institutionalized the breaking effort, making him one of the founders of modern American cryptanalysis. In that era US codebreaking started from zero, and Safford was among the people who started it.

## 一句话

**中文**：他破译的德军潜艇密码（N1），让美国在一战里第一次能读敌人的水下交通。

**English**: The German submarine codes (N1) he broke let America, for the first time in WWI, read the enemy's undersea traffic.
