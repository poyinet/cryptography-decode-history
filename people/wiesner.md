# 斯蒂芬·威斯纳（Stephen Wiesner）

> 人物志 · 💵 · 《共轭编码》作者 · 量子货币与量子密码学的构想者 · 1942–2021 · 美国 / 以色列

## 简介

**中文**：斯蒂芬·威斯纳，美国物理学家，哥伦比亚大学博士，后长期任教于以色列海法大学。约 1970 年写下《共轭编码》，提出量子货币与复用信道等构想——因思想过于超前而屡遭拒稿，沉睡十余年后被贝内特与布拉萨德重新发现，成为量子密码学的源头文献。

**English**: Stephen Wiesner, American-born physicist (PhD, Columbia) who later taught at the University of Haifa. Around 1970 he wrote "Conjugate Coding", proposing quantum money and multiplexed channels — too far ahead of its time to be published, until Bennett and Brassard rediscovered it and made it the founding document of quantum cryptography.

## 一句话

**中文**：威斯纳约 1970 年写成的《共轭编码》手稿接连被期刊拒稿，此后沉睡十余年；直到贝内特与布拉萨德把它从故纸堆里翻出来，才催生出 BB84。这篇「史上被拒稿最久的奠基论文」最终于 1983 年发表在一份计算机理论通讯上。

**English**: Wiesner's "Conjugate Coding", written around 1970, was rejected by journal after journal and slept for over a decade — until Bennett and Brassard dug it out and BB84 was born. One of the foundational papers of quantum information finally appeared in 1983 in a modest computer-theory newsletter.

## 名言

**中文**：量子态测不准、抄不走——这本身就是最诚实的封条。（意译，编者拟）

**English**: "A quantum state cannot be measured without disturbing it, nor copied at all — that is already the most honest seal." (paraphrased)
