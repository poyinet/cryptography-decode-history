# 西尔维奥·米卡利（Silvio Micali）

> 人物志 · 🏆 · 密码学家 · 概率加密/零知识双图灵奖者 · 1955– · 以色列

## 简介

**中文**：意大利裔美国密码学家，可证明安全与零知识证明的共同奠基人。1982 年他和 Goldwasser 用「可交互证明」的形式化定义了两个里程碑：可证明安全的加密和零知识证明。这两项工作把密码学从「相信它安全」升格为「数学上证明它安全」。他和 Goldwasser 的合作延续了几十年——两人 2012 年获图灵奖，表彰的是那篇改变学科的论文。

**English**: An Italian-American cryptographer, co-founding provable security and zero-knowledge proofs. In 1982 he and Goldwasser formalized two milestones via "interactive proofs": provably secure encryption and zero-knowledge proofs. These lifted cryptography from "believe it's secure" to "prove it secure mathematically." His collaboration with Goldwasser lasted decades — the two shared the 2012 Turing Award for that paper that changed the field.

## 一句话

**中文**：他和 Goldwasser 的零知识「三性」证明，是 1989 年 rackoff 形式化的那套理论的源头。

**English**: His and Goldwasser's "three properties" proof of zero knowledge is the source of the theory rackoff formalized in 1989.

## 名言

**中文**：我们把随机性变成信任。

**English**: We take randomness and turn it into trust.
