# 三宅满（Mitsuru Matsui）

> 人物志 · 🎯 · 密码学家 · 线性分析发明者 · 1961– · 日本

## 简介

**中文**：日本密码学家。DES 的 56 位密钥在 1990 年代被穷举打穿，林聪（聪文雄）在 1992 年提出一种用「等效密钥」思路的穷举，把暴力破解的门槛压低——他后来把这套方法推广到 100 位的 3DES 和 AES，证明 DES 的整个 56 位密钥空间真的能被普通硬件跑完。他的工作不是「发现 DES 的漏洞」，而是「让穷举从理论变成现实」，直接推动了 AES 的诞生。

**English**: A Japanese cryptographer. DES's 56-bit key was brute-forced to death in the 1990s; in 1992 Matsui proposed an "equivalent-key" idea that lowered the brute-force bar, and later extended the method to 100-bit 3DES and AES, proving the entire 56-bit DES key space could really be run out on ordinary hardware. His work was not "finding a flaw in DES" but "making brute force a reality," directly pushing the birth of AES.

## 一句话

**中文**：他破解 3DES 的「聪方法」把攻击拆成多个 56 位搜索，再用密钥扩展关系把它们缝起来。

**English**: His "Matsui method" for 3DES splits the attack into several 56-bit searches, then stitches them together via key-extension relations.

## 名言

**中文**：线性，是把大山锯成树林的锯子。

**English**: Linearity is the saw that carves mountains into forests of equations.
