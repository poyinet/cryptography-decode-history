# 文森特·赖伊曼（Vincent Rijmen）

> 人物志 · 🧊 · 密码学家 · AES 联合设计者 · 1975– · 比利时

## 简介

**中文**：比利时密码学家，AES（Rijndael）的两位作者之一。他和 Daemen 从 1996 年起设计 Rijndael，2000 年提交 NIST 征集，经过四轮公开评审后 2001 年被选为 AES。Rijndael 的 SPN 结构（替代-置换网络）与 AES 的 10/12/14 轮设计，至今是分组密码的事实标准。他坚持「算法必须经得起全世界的攻击」，这正是 AES 征集「开放评审」原则的体现。他和 Daemen 的合作，是「两位作者设计一个全球标准」的典范。

**English**: A Belgian cryptographer, one of the two authors of AES (Rijndael). With Daemen, he designed Rijndael from 1996, submitted it to NIST's search in 2000, and after four rounds of public review it was chosen as AES in 2001. Rijndael's SPN structure (substitution-permutation network) and AES's 10/12/14-round design remain the de facto standard for block ciphers today. He insisted "an algorithm must survive the whole world's attacks" — exactly the "open review" principle of the AES search. His collaboration with Daemen is the model of "two authors designing a global standard."

## 一句话

**中文**：AES 的「Rijndael」名字来自两位作者 Rijmen 和 Daemen，算法全名 Rijndael 是两人姓氏的混拼。

**English**: The name "Rijndael" comes from the two authors Rijmen and Daemen — the full name is a blend of their two surnames.

## 名言

**中文**：简单到人人可查证，才是真正的安全。

**English**: Simple enough for anyone to verify — that is real security.
