# 布鲁斯·施奈尔（Bruce Schneier）

> 人物志 · 🧠 · 现代安全思想家 · Blowfish / Twofish 设计者 · 1963– · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：施奈尔是美国密码学家与安全思想家。1994 年出版的《应用密码学》把密码学从学术圈讲给全世界，成为一代程序员与安全工程师的启蒙书；他亲手设计了 Blowfish、Twofish 等算法，其中 Twofish 杀进了 AES 全球决赛五强。此后的三十年里，他以博客与著作持续追问同一个问题：真正的安全不在算法里，而在人与流程之中。

**English**: Schneier is an American cryptographer and security thinker. His 1994 Applied Cryptography carried the field from the ivory tower to the world and became the bible of a generation of engineers; his own designs include Blowfish and Twofish, the latter a finalist in the worldwide AES competition. For thirty years since, his blog and books have hammered one question: real security lives not in algorithms but in people and process.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：《应用密码学》序言里有句名言——「世上有两种密码：一种挡得住你妹妹翻你的文件，一种挡得住大政府」；他运营二十余年的博客「Schneier on Security」是世界上读者最多的安全专栏。

**English**: Fun fact: the preface of Applied Cryptography carries his famous warning that "the right way to learn cryptography is to first learn to forget it"; and his blog, Schneier on Security, running for over two decades, is the most-read security column on the planet.

## 名言

**中文**：「安全是一个过程，而不是一件产品。」（其安全哲学的标志性表述）

**English**: "Security is a process, not a product." (his signature maxim)
