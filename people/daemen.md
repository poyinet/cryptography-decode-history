# 约安·达门（Joan Daemen）

> 人物志 · 🧮 · 比利时密码学家 · AES（Rijndael）共同设计者 · 1965– · 比利时

## 简介

**中文**：达门是比利时密码学家，现为奈梅亨大学教授。1997 至 2000 年，他与文森特·赖门合作设计的 Rijndael 从 NIST 全球征集的 15 个候选算法中胜出，被选为高级加密标准 AES——你今天每一次 HTTPS 连接背后，都是它在一轮轮地「混淆与扩散」。他还参与设计了哈希函数 Keccak，后来被采纳为美国标准 SHA-3。

**English**: Daemen is a Belgian cryptographer, now a professor at Radboud University Nijmegen. In the 1997–2000 NIST competition, the cipher Rijndael he designed with Vincent Rijmen beat fourteen rivals to become the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) — every HTTPS connection you make today rides on its rounds of confusion and diffusion. He also co-designed the hash function Keccak, later standardised as SHA-3.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：「Rijndael」是两位设计者姓氏的拼接——Ri-jn-dael；而达门是唯一一位同时参与设计 AES 与 SHA-3 两大美国国家标准算法的密码学家，堪称「一人双标准」。

**English**: Fun fact: "Rijndael" is a blend of Rijmen and Daemen — Ri-jn-dael. Daemen is the only cryptographer to have co-designed both AES and SHA-3, two US federal standards: one man, two standards.

## 名言

**中文**：「最好的密码，是能用几行字讲清楚、却让攻击者无计可施的密码。」（概括 Rijndael 设计理念）

**English**: "The best cipher is one you can explain in a few lines — and still leaves attackers empty-handed." (a paraphrase of the Rijndael design philosophy)
