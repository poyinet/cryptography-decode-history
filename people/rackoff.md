# 查尔斯·拉科夫（Charles Rackoff）

> 人物志 · 🧩 · 密码学家 · 零知识证明三合伙之一 · 1948– · 加拿大

## 简介

**中文**：以色列密码学家，零知识证明三性的形式化奠基者。1989 年他和 Micali、Goldwasser 一起，把「零知识」这个直觉定义拆成三条可证明的性质：完备性、可信性、零知识性。这个三性框架让零知识从「感觉上安全」变成了「可以证明的协议」，成为后来 ZK-SNARKs 的理论根基。他和 Micali 在可证明安全上的合作，是整个现代密码学「证明安全」这一路的起点。

**English**: An Israeli cryptographer, founding the formalization of zero knowledge's three properties. In 1989, with Micali and Goldwasser, he decomposed the intuition of "zero knowledge" into three provable properties: completeness, soundness, zero knowledge. This three-property framework turned zero knowledge from "seems secure" into "a provable protocol," becoming the theoretical foundation of later ZK-SNARKs. His collaboration with Micali on provable security is the starting point of the whole "prove security" line of modern cryptography.

## 一句话

**中文**：零知识三性的证明里，「零知识性」靠一个「模拟器」证明验证者学不到任何额外信息。

**English**: In the proof of zero knowledge's three properties, "zero-knowledge" rests on a "simulator" proving the verifier learns nothing extra.

## 名言

**中文**：如果证明里没有数字，它多半只是个比喻。

**English**: If the proof contains no numbers, it is probably just a metaphor.
