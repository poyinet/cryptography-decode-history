# 霍斯特·费斯泰尔（Horst Feistel）

> 人物志 · 🧱 · Feistel 网络发明人 · DES 的核心设计者 · 1915–1990 · 德裔美国人

## 简介

**中文**：霍斯特·费斯泰尔是德裔美国密码学家。他 1934 年移居美国，1960 年代加入 IBM 沃森研究中心，开始研究分组密码。他发明的 Feistel 网络把数据分成两半、经多轮函数混合，成为此后几乎所有分组密码的通用骨架；基于这一结构，他与团队设计出 Lucifer，并最终演化成美国国家标准 DES。在 AES 问世之前，DES 统治全球加密标准数十年。

**English**: Horst Feistel was a German-born American cryptographer. He emigrated to the United States in 1934 and joined IBM's Watson Research Center in the 1960s, where he pioneered the field of block ciphers. His Feistel network — splitting data into two halves and mixing them through repeated rounds — became the universal skeleton of nearly every block cipher that followed. Building on it, he and his IBM colleagues created the Lucifer cipher, which evolved into DES, adopted as the U.S. federal encryption standard and used worldwide for decades until AES replaced it.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：IBM 团队最初的 Lucifer 使用 128 位密钥，而最终标准化的 DES 只有 56 位——外界长期怀疑是 NSA 在幕后要求削弱；这场「密钥之争」让 DES 带着后门疑云统治世界数十年，也开启了密码学界对政府标准的永恒争论。

**English**: Fun fact: IBM's original Lucifer used 128-bit keys, but the standardized DES kept only 56 — a cut widely suspected to have been demanded by the NSA. That suspicion shadowed DES for decades and opened the everlasting debate over government-designed standards.

## 名言

**中文**：把数据一分为二，让每一半都成为另一半的锁。（意译，编者拟）

**English**: Split the data in two, and let each half be the other's lock. (paraphrased)
