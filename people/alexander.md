# 休·亚历山大（Conel Hugh O'Donel Alexander）

> 人物志 · ♟️ · 国际象棋大师 · Hut 8 破译 Enigma 的核心数学家 · 1909–1974 · 英国（爱尔兰出生）

## 简介

**中文**：休·亚历山大是英国国际象棋大师与数学家。1938 年他夺得英国国际象棋冠军，1940 年应征进入布莱切利园，加入图灵的 Hut 8 小组，参与攻克最难啃的德国海军 Enigma——1941 年 5 月击沉俾斯麦号所依赖的破译情报，就出自他所在团队的成果。图灵 1942 年赴美后，他接任 Hut 8 负责人直至战争结束。战后他投身计算机行业，先后任职于约翰·刘易斯百货与英国电气公司，成为英国计算机产业的先驱。

**English**: Hugh Alexander was an Irish-born British chess master and mathematician. He won the British Chess Championship in 1938, and was recruited to Bletchley Park in 1940. In Alan Turing's Hut 8 he tackled the hardest target of all — German naval Enigma — and the section's breakthroughs helped sink the battleship Bismarck in May 1941. When Turing left for the United States in 1942, Alexander took over as head of Hut 8 and led it until the war's end. Afterward he carried computing into British industry, working for the John Lewis Partnership and English Electric, and became a pioneer of the nation's computer industry.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：亚历山大战后仍是英格兰棋坛的顶梁柱——他曾多次代表英国出战国际象棋奥林匹克赛，还著有研究棋王阿廖欣的名作，是少有的以密码学家与棋手双重身份名留青史的人物。

**English**: Fun fact: Alexander stayed a force in chess for life — he represented England at the Chess Olympiads and wrote a celebrated study of Alekhine, carving his name into both cryptology and chess history.

**English**: Breaking naval Enigma was like chess: learn how the machine moves, then count every one of its variations. (paraphrased)
