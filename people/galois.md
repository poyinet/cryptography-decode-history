# 埃瓦里斯特·伽罗瓦（Évariste Galois）

> 人物志 · 🪞 · 数学家 · 群论创立者 · 1811–1832 · 法国

## 简介

**中文**：法国少年天才，21 岁死于决斗，却为数学留下「伽罗瓦理论」。他把多项式方程的对称性抽象成「群」，并构造出有限域（今天写作 GF(p) 的域）。这套域上算术如今是 AES 的地基——AES 的 SubBytes 字节替换、Galois 计数模式的乘法，全在他当年手稿的有限域上进行。一个因恋爱纠纷决斗而死的人，撑起了你手机里每一段加密。

**English**: A French prodigy killed in a duel at 21, who left mathematics "Galois theory". He abstracted the symmetry of polynomial equations into "groups" and constructed finite fields (what we write as GF(p)). That field arithmetic is the bedrock of AES today — AES's SubBytes substitution and the multiplication in Galois/Counter Mode all live in the finite fields of his notebooks. A man dead from a duel over a romance holds up every encryption on your phone.

## 一句话

**中文**：他临死前夜把全部手稿塞进信里寄给友人；第二天决斗身亡。伽罗瓦理论因此有「最后一夜」的传奇。

**English**: He stuffed all his notebooks into a letter the night before dying; he was killed in a duel the next day. Galois theory earned its "final night" legend that way.
