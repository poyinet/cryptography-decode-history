# 查尔斯·巴贝奇（Charles Babbage）

> 人物志 · ⚙️ · 差分机与分析机的设计者 · 维吉尼亚密码的「失名英雄」 · 1791–1871 · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：查尔斯·巴贝奇是英国数学家与工程师。1830 年代他设计「分析机」，一台以打孔卡驱动的可编程机械计算机，被视为计算机思想的先驱。1854 年，他在破解维吉尼亚密码时发现了与卡西斯基检验法等价的方法：借助密文中重复片段的间距推算密钥长度。但因保密要求他从未发表，成果埋没于笔记，直到 1985 年才被学者重新发现——功劳早已记在九年后出版的卡西斯基名下。

**English**: Charles Babbage was an English mathematician and engineer. In the 1830s he designed the Analytical Engine, a programmable mechanical computer driven by punched cards, and is regarded as a founding visionary of computing. Around 1854, while dabbling in ciphers, he broke the Vigenère cipher using a method equivalent to the Kasiski examination. Bound by a promise of secrecy, he never published, and the breakthrough lay buried in his notebooks until historians rediscovered it in 1985. By then, the credit had long since gone to Kasiski.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：让巴贝奇出手的是一桩「发明权之争」——牙医思韦茨 1854 年宣称发明了新密码，巴贝奇一眼认出那是维吉尼亚密码并当场破解；这场笔战的手稿，成了他破译功绩的唯一证据。

**English**: Fun fact: Babbage tackled the Vigenère because of a priority dispute — in 1854 a dentist named J.H.B. Thwaites claimed to have invented a "new cipher." Babbage recognised it as the Vigenère and cracked it on the spot; the manuscripts of that exchange are the only record of his feat.

## 名言

**中文**：「算术的全部，如今看来都可以交给机器。」

**English**: "The whole of arithmetic now appeared within the grasp of mechanism."
