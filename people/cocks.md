# 克利福德·科克斯（Clifford Cocks）

> 人物志 · 🇬🇧 · 秘密发明 RSA 等价算法的 GCHQ 数学家 · 1950– · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：克利福德·科克斯是英国 GCHQ 的数学家。1973 年，年仅 23 岁的他在同事埃利斯「非秘密加密」构想的基础上，独立发明了与 RSA 等价的非对称加密算法——比公开的 RSA 早了整整四年。受英国《官方保密法》约束，这一成果从未发表，直到 1997 年才获准解密。此时 RSA 早已成为互联网加密的基石，他只能以「隐秘先驱」的身份被历史追认。

**English**: Clifford Cocks is a British mathematician who spent his career at GCHQ. In 1973, aged just 23, building on James Ellis's concept of "non-secret encryption," he devised an asymmetric cryptosystem mathematically equivalent to RSA — a full four years before RSA was published. Bound by the Official Secrets Act, the work stayed classified until 1997. By then RSA had conquered the world; Cocks was recognised only in retrospect.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：科克斯的方案与 RSA 殊途同归——都建立在欧拉定理与因式分解的困难之上；但他的论文从未发表，直到 1997 年 GCHQ 解密，密码学界才发现「RSA 的原型」早已秘密存在了 24 年。

**English**: Fun fact: Cocks's scheme and RSA converged on the same mathematics — Euler's theorem and the difficulty of factoring. But his paper was never published; when GCHQ declassified it in 1997, the community discovered that a prototype of RSA had existed, in secret, for 24 years.

## 名言

**中文**：我的工作不能署名，但数学本身会记住谁先到达。（意译，编者拟）

**English**: My work could never carry my name, but mathematics itself remembers who arrived first. (paraphrased)
