# 弗劳尔斯（Flowers）

> 人物志 · 💡 · 英国邮局研究站（Dollis Hill）工程师 · Colossus 设计师 · 1905-1998 · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：汤米·弗劳尔斯在伦敦邮局研究站做电话交换工程，二战中被调往布莱切利园。别人担心电子管不可靠，他从电话交换机的实践里知道它们可以做得足够稳定。他设计的 Colossus 是世界第一台可编程电子计算机，在诺曼底登陆前把 Tunny 破译提速到每秒五千字符。战后机器被销毁、秘密守了三十年；晚年的他亲眼看到 Colossus 被重建、被承认——1998 年他去世，正好赶上这段历史解密。

**English**: Tommy Flowers was a telephone-exchange engineer at the GPO research station at Dollis Hill, drafted to Bletchley Park during the war. While others doubted valves, his exchange practice told him they could be made reliable. His Colossus — the world's first programmable electronic computer — sped Tunny breaking up to 5,000 characters per second before D-Day. After the war the machine was destroyed and the secret kept for thirty years; in his final years he lived to see Colossus rebuilt and acknowledged — he died in 1998, just as the story came out.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：弗劳尔斯坚持用电子管是因为电话交换机的经验——别人担心不可靠，他知道可以；Colossus 战后被拆毁，秘密守到 1970 年代。

**English**: Fun fact: Flowers trusted valves from telephone exchanges; Colossus was dismantled after the war and the secret held until the 1970s.

## 名言

**中文**：「电话交换的经验告诉我：电子管可以做到可靠——这台机器必须是电子的。」

**English**: "My telephone-exchange experience told me valves could be made reliable — the machine had to be electronic."
