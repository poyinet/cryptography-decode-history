# 约瑟夫·毛博涅（Joseph Mauborgne）

> 人物志 · 🎲 · 美国陆军通信兵 · 一次性密码本（OTP）的共同发明者 · 1881–1971 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：毛博涅是美国陆军通信兵军官，军旅生涯的最后四年担任全军通信兵主任。1917 年，他与 AT&T 工程师弗纳姆合作，给弗纳姆的密钥流电报系统补上最关键的一环：密钥必须随机、与明文等长、并且永不重复使用——「一次性密码本」就此诞生，成为历史上第一种被数学证明绝对不可破解的密码。他还与希特上尉一道独立重造了杰斐逊转轮，其成果演化为 1922 年列装的 M-94。

**English**: Mauborgne was a US Army Signal Corps officer who capped his career as Chief Signal Officer. In 1917, working with AT&T engineer Gilbert Vernam, he added the decisive piece to Vernam's keystream telegraph: the key must be random, as long as the message, and never reused — the one-time pad, the first cipher ever proven mathematically unbreakable. He and Captain Parker Hitt also independently rebuilt the Jefferson wheel, which evolved into the Army's M-94 of 1922.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：弗纳姆的原始系统靠重复密钥带工作，毛博涅坚持用随机密钥「用一次就扔」——而香农直到 1949 年才用信息论证明他们做对了；更妙的是，美国国务院 1919 年也独立发明过同款密码，历史上一共「撞车」了至少三次。

**English**: Fun fact: Vernam's original system reused its key tape; Mauborgne insisted on random, single-use keys — and Shannon only proved them right with information theory in 1949. The US State Department had even independently invented the same cipher in 1919: history staged the OTP at least three times.

## 名言

**中文**：「密钥必须随机、与明文等长、并且只用一次——少一样，都不是完美保密。」（概括 OTP 三原则）

**English**: "The key must be random, as long as the plaintext, and used exactly once — miss any one, and it is no longer perfect secrecy." (a paraphrase of the OTP principle)
