# 托马斯·杰斐逊（Thomas Jefferson）

> 人物志 · 🏛️ · 美国第三任总统 · 「杰斐逊转轮」发明者 · 1743–1826 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：杰斐逊是《独立宣言》起草人、美国第三任总统，也是一位隐秘的密码爱好者。1790 年代他设计出「杰斐逊转轮」——26 片乱序字母圆盘套在同一轴上，转动圆盘拼出明文、从另一行读出密文。他至死没有公布这件作品，直到 120 年后美国陆军独立重造出同款装置，以 M-94 之名列装，杰斐逊的「转轮」才被追认为现代轮式密码机的始祖。

**English**: Jefferson drafted the Declaration of Independence and served as the third US President — but he was also a secret cipher hobbyist. In the 1790s he designed a wheel cipher: 26 wooden disks, each carrying a scrambled alphabet, threaded on one axle; turn the disks to spell the plaintext across one row and read the ciphertext from another. He never published it. Only 120 years later, when the US Army independently re-invented the same device and adopted it as the M-94, was Jefferson's wheel recognised as the ancestor of all wheel ciphers.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：杰斐逊的转轮在手稿里躺了一百多年，美国陆军 1922 年列装的 M-94 是工程师们的「独立再发明」——发明人本人对此一无所知，直到 1920 年代学者翻出他的手稿，历史才补上了这一课。

**English**: Fun fact: Jefferson's wheel lay in his papers for over a century while the US Army's M-94, adopted in 1922, was an independent re-invention by engineers who had never heard of him; only when scholars found his manuscripts in the 1920s did history connect the dots.

## 名言

**中文**：「我终于制成了一种我认为绝对可靠的密码。」——致罗伯特·帕特森的信（1802）

**English**: "I have at length produced a cipher which I think infallible." — letter to Robert Patterson, 1802
