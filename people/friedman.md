# 弗里德曼（Friedman）

> 人物志 · 🈁 · 美国陆军信号情报处（SIS）密码学家 · 破解 Purple 的团队领袖 · 1891-1969 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：威廉·弗里德曼是美国密码学的奠基人。他早年研究遗传学，把统计思维带进密码分析；一战时为军方效力，此后用二十年把陆军 SIS 建成世界一流的破译机构。1940 年，他的团队在没有实机的情况下逆向出日本 Purple 密码机的结构。长期超负荷工作让他在破译完成前后病倒，却也留下了整整一代美国密码学家。战后他参与起草美国密码政策的基石文件，被称为「美国密码学之父」。

**English**: William Friedman was the founder of American cryptology. Trained in genetics, he brought statistical thinking into cryptanalysis; after serving in WWI he spent twenty years building the Army's SIS into a world-class codebreaking establishment. In 1940 his team reverse-engineered the structure of Japan's Purple machine without ever seeing one. Years of overwork broke his health just as the break was completed — but he had already trained a whole generation of American cryptanalysts. After the war he helped draft the founding documents of US signals policy, earning the title "father of American cryptology."

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：弗里德曼最初学的是遗传学，把统计思维带进密码分析；他一生破译过 8,000 多份密电，还参与起草了美国密码政策基石。

**English**: Fun fact: Friedman trained in genetics and brought statistics to cryptanalysis; he broke over 8,000 messages and helped draft US signals policy.
