# 阿图尔·谢尔比乌斯（Arthur Scherbius）

> 人物志 · 🗝️ · Enigma 转子密码机的发明者 · 1878–1929 · 德国

## 简介

**中文**：阿图尔·谢尔比乌斯是德国电气工程师。1918 年，他申请了转子密码机的专利：可更换的转子组与插线板使字母替换的复杂度空前提高，并创办公司将其商品化，最初面向商业客户。1926 年起德国海军、陆军、空军相继列装，Enigma 成为二战德军的核心密码机。他 1929 年死于马车事故，没能看到自己的机器成为战争的主角。

**English**: Arthur Scherbius was a German electrical engineer. In 1918 he patented a cipher machine built on rotors, and founded a company to sell it, first to the commercial market. From 1926 onward the German Navy, Army and Air Force adopted it in turn, and Enigma became the Wehrmacht's central cipher machine of the Second World War. Scherbius died in a carriage accident in 1929 and never lived to see his machine become the war's most famous cryptosystem.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：谢尔比乌斯的 Enigma 起初是卖给银行的商业产品，广告称其「保密性无可匹敌」；德军列装后，波兰密码局很快盯上了它——而发明人本人 1929 年就死于一场马车事故，年仅 50 岁。

**English**: Fun fact: Enigma was first marketed to banks as a commercial product with ads boasting "unrivalled secrecy." The German military adopted it in 1926; Polish cryptanalysts were studying it within years — while Scherbius himself died in a carriage accident in 1929 at fifty.

## 名言

**中文**：「Enigma」在德语里意为「谜」——他给机器起的名字，成了战争中最难解的谜题。

**English**: "Enigma" means "riddle" in German — the name he gave his machine became the hardest riddle of the war.
