# 凯撒（Caesar）

> 人物志 · 🏛️ · 罗马统帅 · 政治家与军事家 · 公元前 100–前 44 · 罗马

## 简介

**中文**：盖乌斯·尤利乌斯·凯撒，罗马共和国末期最杰出的统帅与政治家，八年征服高卢。据苏埃托尼乌斯《罗马十二帝王传》记载，他以字母移位书写机密军令，成为历史上第一个留下名字的密码使用者。公元前 44 年遇刺身亡，「我来，我见，我征服」与凯撒密码一同流传至今。

**English**: Gaius Julius Caesar, the greatest general and statesman of the late Roman Republic, conquered Gaul in eight years. According to Suetonius, he wrote confidential orders by shifting the letters of the alphabet — the first named cipher user in history. Assassinated in 44 BC, he left the world both "Veni, vidi, vici" and the Caesar cipher.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：凯撒密码的偏移量其实不确定——史载他用后移 3 位；「凯撒密码」之名是后世起的。

**English**: Fun fact: Caesar's shift was not fixed — he usually wrote three places on; the name "Caesar cipher" was coined later.

## 名言

**中文**：「我来，我见，我征服。—— Veni, vidi, vici」

**English**: "I came, I saw, I conquered. — Veni, vidi, vici"
