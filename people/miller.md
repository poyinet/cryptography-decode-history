# 弗兰克·米勒（Frank Miller）

> 人物志 · 📜 · 银行家 · 一次性密钥本先驱 · 1842–1911 · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：得克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校的数学家。真正让他名留史册的不是椭圆曲线，而是 1976 年他与 Rabin 提出的「Miller-Rabin 素性检验」：一种用随机数快速判定一个大数是不是素数的方法。RSA 要安全，就得找大素数；而 Miller-Rabin 让一台机器几秒内就能确认「这个数几乎肯定是素数」，成为所有公钥系统生成密钥的标配。它至今仍在 OpenSSL、GnuPG、比特币里运行。当年他只是在会议上交了薄薄几页的摘要，没有正式发表，却成了整个公钥密码的底层地基。

**English**: A mathematician at the University of Texas at Austin. What really made his name is not elliptic curves but the "Miller-Rabin primality test" he and Rabin proposed in 1976: a method that uses random numbers to quickly decide whether a big number is prime. RSA's security depends on finding big primes; Miller-Rabin lets a machine confirm in seconds "this number is almost certainly prime," becoming the standard key-generation step in every public-key system. It still runs inside OpenSSL, GnuPG, and Bitcoin today. Back then he only submitted a few pages of abstract at a conference, never formally published, yet it became the bedrock of all public-key cryptography.

## 一句话

**中文**：Miller-Rabin 素性检验只靠随机数就能判断素数，是所有 RSA 密钥生成算法的地基。

**English**: The Miller-Rabin primality test decides primality with random numbers alone — it is the bedrock of every RSA key-generation algorithm.

## 名言

**中文**：曲线上的点，藏着最快的锁。

**English**: The fastest locks hide among points on a curve.
