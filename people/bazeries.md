# 艾蒂安·巴泽里（Étienne Bazeries）

> 人物志 · 🔍 · 法国军事情报破译专家 · 路易十四「大密码」的破解者 · 1846–1931 · 法国

## 简介

**中文**：巴泽里是法国陆军军官、自学成才的破译高手。1893 年，他攻破了罗西尼奥尔家族为路易十四打造、沉睡近两百年的「大密码」，读出了太阳王的私人信件——其中竟藏着一桩宫廷悬案：那位神秘「铁面人」的下落。他还发明了巴泽里转筒密码机，并卷入了世纪之交最轰动的案子：德雷福斯事件中，他公开指认军方呈堂的密件里有伪证。

**English**: Bazeries was a French army officer and a self-taught codebreaker. In 1893 he cracked the "Great Cipher" of the Rossignol family, used by Louis XIV and unread for nearly two centuries, and read the Sun King's private letters — including the court mystery of the Man in the Iron Mask. He also invented the Bazeries cylinder cipher device, and in the century's most explosive affair he testified that documents the army had used to convict Dreyfus were forgeries.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：他自称从「铁面人」的密信里读出了真相——那位神秘囚徒是将军维维安·德·比隆德，这个说法至今仍在历史学界争论；而他发明的巴泽里转筒，正是杰斐逊转轮、M-94 这条轮式密码家族谱系上的法国分支。

**English**: Fun fact: he claimed the secret letters revealed the Man in the Iron Mask as General Vivien de Bulonde — a theory historians still debate — and his Bazeries cylinder is the French branch of the same wheel-cipher family that runs from Jefferson to the M-94.

## 名言

**中文**：「沉睡了两百年的太阳王密信，终于等到了肯读它的人。」（概括其破译事迹）

**English**: "After two centuries, the Sun King's secret letters finally found a reader willing to read them." (a paraphrase of his feat)
