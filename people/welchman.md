# 韦尔奇曼（Welchman）

> 人物志 · 🧩 · 数学家 · 对角线板的发明者 · 1906–1985 · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：剑桥几何学家，布莱切利园破译体系的缔造者之一，与图灵、Joan Clarke 同属 8 号木屋。图为让 Bombe 跑得更快，他提出了「对角线板」（对角化矩阵）：与其对每个转子设置从头算起，不如预先算出那张对角表，一次把候选压缩到极小，搜索时间从数小时压到十几分钟。没有这块板，Bombe 只能算个实验室玩具，成了真正的战争机器。他后来主持了布莱切利园的密码分析体系设计，战后在 GCHQ 工作至退休，是「把数学破译变成可运转工厂」的工程师。

**English**: A Cambridge geometer and one of the architects of Bletchley Park's codebreaking organisation, in Hut 8 alongside Turing and Joan Clarke. To make the Bombe run faster, he proposed the "diagonal board" (diagonalizing matrix): instead of recomputing from scratch for each rotor setting, pre-compute that diagonal table once to compress the candidates to a tiny set, cutting search time from hours to a dozen minutes. Without that board the Bombe would have stayed a lab curiosity, not a true war machine. He later led the design of Bletchley's cryptanalytic system and served at GCHQ until retirement — the engineer who "turned mathematical codebreaking into a working factory."

## 一句话

**中文**：「对角线板」是 Welchman 给 Bombe 加的加速层，把搜索从数小时压到十几分钟——Bombe 因此成了战争机器。

**English**: The "diagonal board" is the accelerator Welchman added to the Bombe, cutting search from hours to a dozen minutes — that is how the Bombe became a war machine.

## 名言

**中文**：机器负责搜索，人负责思考。

**English**: Machines do the searching; people do the thinking.
