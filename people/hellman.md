# 马丁·赫尔曼（Martin Hellman）

> 人物志 · 🤝 · 公钥密码学奠基人 · Diffie-Hellman 密钥交换提出者 · 1945– · 美国

## 简介

**中文**：马丁·赫尔曼是美国密码学家、斯坦福大学教授。1976 年，他与惠特菲尔德·迪菲发表划时代论文《密码学的新方向》，提出 Diffie-Hellman 密钥交换：双方在公开信道上即可协商出共享密钥，无需任何事先的秘密约定。这篇论文宣告公钥密码学的诞生，直接启发了 RSA 等后续体制，他本人也于 2015 年与迪菲同获图灵奖。晚年的他把目光转向核风险与全球安全，认为「和平」与密码学一样值得精心守护。

**English**: Martin Hellman is an American cryptographer and Stanford professor. In 1976, with Whitfield Diffie, he published "New Directions in Cryptography," proposing the Diffie–Hellman key exchange: two parties negotiate a shared secret over a public channel, with no prior secret meeting. The paper gave birth to public-key cryptography and inspired the RSA system that followed; its scheme remains the basis of secure key exchange on the internet today. For this work he and Diffie shared the 2015 Turing Award. In later life Hellman turned to nuclear-risk analysis and global security, arguing, together with his wife Dorothie, that peace deserves the same careful engineering as cryptography.

## 一句话

**中文**：冷知识：1975 年，美国国家安全局曾施压要求撤回迪菲-赫尔曼的论文，就此拉开「密码战争」的序幕——可论文最终还是照常发表，公钥密码也随之传遍世界。

**English**: Fun fact: in 1975 the NSA pressed to have Diffie and Hellman's work suppressed — the opening shot of the "Crypto Wars." The paper was published anyway, and public-key cryptography swept the world.

## 名言

**中文**：密码学证明了一件事：陌生人之间，也能共享一个只有彼此知道的秘密。

**English**: Cryptography proved that even strangers can share a secret known only to the two of them.
