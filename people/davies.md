# 唐纳德·戴维斯（Donald Davies）

> 人物志 · 🧬 · 英国计算机科学家 · 分组密码先驱 · 1924–2000 · 英国

## 简介

**中文**：英国情报机构（GCHQ）的数学家。1974 年他在一场内部研讨会上独立推出了公钥密码的核心思想——两人共享密钥而不需要面对面交换——比 Diffie-Hellman 公开发表早了两年。只是那篇成果被当成机密雪藏了二十年，直到 1996 年才解密，让他「先想到、后署名」的命运成为公钥史上最大的遗憾之一。

**English**: A mathematician at GCHQ. In 1974, at an internal seminar, he independently worked out the core idea of public-key crypto — two parties sharing a key without ever meeting — two years before Diffie-Hellman went public. The result stayed classified for twenty years, until a 1996 declassification made his "thought it first, credited it later" fate one of the biggest regrets in public-key history.

## 一句话

**中文**：他和 Cocks、Williamson 同为 GCHQ「公钥三人组」：三人都早于 RSA 想通了公钥原理，却都被保密制度压了头。

**English**: He, Cocks and Williamson were GCHQ's "public-key trio": all three had worked out public-key cryptography before RSA, all buried by classification.
