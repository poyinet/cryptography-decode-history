# 齐默尔曼电报（The Zimmermann Telegram）

> 密件册 · 1917 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`adfgvx`

## 说明

**中文**：齐默尔曼电报：1917 年 1 月德国外长密令墨西哥进攻美国的电文。伦敦海军部 40 号房破译它之后，美国加入了第一次世界大战。

**English**: The Zimmermann Telegram: January 1917, the German Foreign Secretary secretly urging Mexico to attack America. After Room 40 in London decrypted it, the United States entered the First World War.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：—— 齐默尔曼电报（1917 年 1 月 16 日，德译英节选）

「我们打算于二月一日起开始无限制潜艇战。尽管如此，我们将设法使美利坚合众国保持中立。倘若此事不能成功，我们提议与墨西哥结盟，基础如下：共同作战、共同媾和、慷慨的财政支持，以及我方谅解墨西哥将收复其在得克萨斯、新墨西哥与亚利桑那的失地。细节由你酌定。一旦与美国开战确凿无疑，请即刻将上述内容极其秘密地知会贵国总统，并建议他主动邀请日本立即加入，同时由我们在日墨之间斡旋。」

🤔 反事实思考：如果这份电报晚发一个月——美国已经因潜艇袭击而愤怒参战——它就只是一份普通的外交文件，而非改变战争走向的关键。历史的转折，往往取决于时机与破译的巧合。

**English**: — the Zimmermann Telegram (16 January 1917, translated excerpt)

"WE INTEND TO BEGIN ON THE FIRST OF FEBRUARY UNRESTRICTED SUBMARINE WARFARE. WE SHALL ENDEAVOR IN SPITE OF THIS TO KEEP THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA NEUTRAL. IN THE EVENT OF THIS NOT SUCCEEDING, WE MAKE MEXICO A PROPOSAL OF ALLIANCE ON THE FOLLOWING BASIS: MAKE WAR TOGETHER, MAKE PEACE TOGETHER, GENEROUS FINANCIAL SUPPORT, AND AN UNDERSTANDING ON OUR PART THAT MEXICO IS TO RECONQUER HER LOST TERRITORY IN TEXAS, NEW MEXICO, AND ARIZONA. THE SETTLEMENT IN DETAIL IS LEFT TO YOU. YOU WILL INFORM THE PRESIDENT OF THE ABOVE MOST SECRETLY AS SOON AS THE OUTBREAK OF WAR WITH THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA IS CERTAIN, AND ADD THE SUGGESTION THAT HE SHOULD, ON HIS OWN INITIATIVE, INVITE JAPAN TO IMMEDIATE ADHERENCE AND AT THE SAME TIME MEDIATE BETWEEN JAPAN AND OURSELVES."

🤔 What if: Sent a month later — after America had already entered the war over unrestricted submarine warfare — this telegram would have been just another diplomatic document. The turning of history often hinges on timing and decryption converging.
