# 半部电台——红军第一部电台（The Half Radio — the Red Army’s First Set）

> 密件册 · 1930 · 龙冈战斗 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`morse`

## 说明

**中文**：1930 年 12 月龙冈一仗，红军缴获国民党军第 18 师电台一部：发报机已损，唯收报机完好——“半部电台起家”。1931 年 1 月红军第一支无线电队成立，王诤任队长；同年开办无线电训练班，并由此开辟了收报、侦听与密码破译之路。

**English**: At Longgang in December 1930 the Red Army captured one radio from the KMT 18th Division — the transmitter broken, the receiver intact: “a half radio to start with”. In January 1931 the first radio corps was formed under Wang Zheng, followed by a training school that opened the road to interception, radio intelligence and cryptanalysis.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：1930 年 12 月 30 日龙冈战斗后，红军战士从战利品中挑出了一部电台：发报机摔坏，收报机还能用。毛泽东、朱德下令组建无线电队，王诤任队长——他后来成为人民军队通信事业的创建人。1931 年 1 月红军第一支无线电队在宁都成立，接着办起无线电训练班，三个月成报务员。从此，敌台的电波成为苏区的“千里眼”“顺风耳”——而密码破译，就是从这些截获的电报里开始的。

【史料性质】实存 · 1930—1931 年。正文为依史重构的通信日志片段。

**English**: After the Battle of Longgang on 30 December 1930, Red Army soldiers picked one radio out of the spoils: the transmitter was smashed, the receiver still worked. Mao Zedong and Zhu De ordered a radio corps formed, with Wang Zheng as its commander — he would become the founder of PLA communications. In January 1931 the first corps was established near Ningdu, and a training school soon produced operators in three months. From then on enemy airwaves became the Red Army’s “far-seeing eyes and far-hearing ears” — and cryptanalysis began with these intercepted messages.
