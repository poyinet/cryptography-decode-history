# 红军无线电密码（Red Army Radio Ciphers）

> 密件册 · 1931 · 中央苏区 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`freq`

## 说明

**中文**：拥有“看不见的电台”后，红军很快走上侦听破译之路：编印口令本、把握敌台呼号与频率规律、以加密电报的报文构筑“密中之密”。长征途中，军委二局的破译成果被誉为“令我们行军时多了一双眼睛”。以下为依史重构的敌台记录页。

**English**: Once the “invisible radios” were in hand, the Red Army moved quickly into interception and decryption: code-books, call-sign patterns, frequency discipline — the cipher within the cipher. On the Long March, decrypted traffic from the General Staff bureau warned the column like a second pair of eyes. Below, one reconstructed page of intercepted traffic.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：截获记录（依史重构）：
1931 · 某师电台（呼号 …）
09:14 来电：明码前段“本师明□□日向□□集结”
09:19 换用本师密码本发电——同日两电，一明一密，报务员值班记录本上留着两行对照。

红军无线电侦察的要诀：报务员听呼叫号与拍发手法，测向与核对日程，密码员再破密文。呼叫号与手法本身，就是破译的第一层钥匙。

【史料性质】实存事物（1931 年红军无线电侦察），正文为示意重构。

**English**: Interception log (reconstructed for illustration):
1931 · Division station (call sign …)
09:14 message: plaintext prefix “the division will assemble at …”
09:19 new message in the division code-book — same day, one plain, one enciphered, two lines side by side in the operator’s log.

The essentials of Red Army radio intelligence: operators learn call signs and fist, direction-finding and schedules; code clerks then crack the cipher. The call sign and the fist are the first keys.

[Nature] Real practice (1931 Red Army radio intelligence); text is a schematic reconstruction.
