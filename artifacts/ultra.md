# Ultra 战报（Ultra Briefing）

> 密件册 · 1940-45 · 性质：依据史实的剧化 / Dramatized · 关联游戏：`enigma`

## 说明

**中文**：布莱切利园 Ultra 战报：标记「TOP SECRET ULTRA」的情报摘要，全部源于对德军 Enigma 密电的实时破译。

**English**: An Ultra briefing from Bletchley Park: an intelligence summary marked TOP SECRET ULTRA, born from the live decryption of German Enigma traffic.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：绝密 —— ULTRA
发自：布莱切利园 · 3 号木屋
呈报：战时内阁 · 特别分发

1.【海军】潜艇群奉命于 0400 时前抵达 AK41 方格；护航队改道，绕开该海域。
2.【陆军】Enigma 报告隆美尔补给船队：3 艘运输船、2 艘护航舰，预计 48 小时内抵达。批准拦截——请以空中掩护作为掩护。
3.【空军】截获夜间战斗机信号链；预计 11 日 2330 时空袭英格兰中部。
4.【来源纪律】任何行动不得暴露情报来源。若敌人必须知道我们知情，就必须让他们看到我们用别的方式得知。

分发范围：首相与帝国总参谋长。阅后即焚。

🤔 反事实思考：如果 ULTRA 泄露了哪怕一封电报，德军就会更换全部密钥体系——盟军将失去整场战争中最关键的情报优势。丘吉尔战后写道：「胜利属于秘密。」这份秘密的重量，由布莱切利园的一万名沉默者共同承担。

**English**: TOP SECRET — ULTRA
FROM: Bletchley Park, Hut 3
TO: War Cabinet — Special Distribution

1. NAVAL: U-boat group ordered to grid AK 41 by 0400 hrs; reroute the convoy clear of the box.
2. ARMY: Rommel's convoy reported by Enigma — 3 transports, 2 escorts, ETA 48 hrs. Interception authorised; use air cover as the cover.
3. AIR: Night-fighter signal chain intercepted; raid expected over the Midlands, 11th, 2330 hrs.
4. SOURCE RULE: No action may betray the source. If the enemy must learn that we know, we must be seen to learn it another way.

DISTRIBUTION: PM and CIGS only. Burn after reading.

🤔 What if: A single leaked ULTRA decrypt would have forced the Germans to change every key — and the Allies would have lost their most decisive advantage. Churchill understood: victory belonged to the secret, and its weight rested on ten thousand silent shoulders at Bletchley Park.
