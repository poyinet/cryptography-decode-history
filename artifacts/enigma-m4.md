# Enigma M4 海军四转子密码机（The Enigma M4 (Naval)）

> 密件册 · 1942 年 · 大西洋 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`enigma`

## 说明

**中文**：1942 年初，德国海军为 U 型潜艇装备四转子 Enigma M4，在标准三转子之外增设希腊字母转子，使密钥空间暴增。大西洋狼群战术因此一度畅行无阻，盟军直到年底才借助缴获的密钥资料攻破 M4，扭转护航战局。

**English**: In early 1942, the German Navy equipped U-boats with the four-rotor Enigma M4, adding a Greek-letter rotor to the standard three and vastly expanding the key space. The wolf-pack tactic flourished until the Allies finally broke the M4 late that year, turning the tide of convoy warfare.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：三转子 Enigma 被破译后，德国海军于 1942 年 2 月启用 M4：额外的一个转子让组合数增长 26 倍，并专门用于潜艇通信。U 型潜艇一度在北大西洋重创盟军船队，每月击沉数十万吨运输船。直到 1942 年 10 月，英军从 U-559 号潜艇上缴获 M4 密钥与短信号本，布莱切利园才重新掌握海神密码的钥匙，大西洋之战自此转折。

**English**: After the three-rotor Enigma was broken, the German Navy introduced the M4 in February 1942: one extra rotor multiplied the combinations by 26 and was reserved for submarine traffic. U-boats devastated Allied convoys in the North Atlantic, sinking hundreds of thousands of tons of shipping each month. Not until October 1942, when British sailors captured M4 key materials and the short-signal book from U-559, did Bletchley Park regain the key to the Triton cipher — and the Battle of the Atlantic turned.
