# 萨默顿人案（Tamam Shud / Somerton Man）

> 密件册 · 1948 年 · 澳大利亚阿德莱德 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`detective`

## 说明

**中文**：1948 年澳大利亚萨默顿海滩发现一具无名男尸，衣袋里藏着撕自《鲁拜集》的纸条，上书波斯语「Tamam Shud」（已结束），书中夹页更留有密码；半个多世纪过去，死者身份、死因与密码含义至今成谜，为澳洲最著名的悬案。

**English**: In 1948 a well-dressed man was found dead on Somerton Beach, Adelaide; in his pocket was a scrap torn from a Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam reading "Tamam Shud" (Persian for "it is ended"), and a copy of the book found later bore a cipher. More than seventy years on, his identity, cause of death and the cipher's meaning remain unknown — Australia's most celebrated cold case.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：男尸口袋中的纸条写着「Tamam Shud」——波斯语「已结束」。警方循线找到原书，书末印着电话号码，书后空白页上留下一行五组字母的密码，下方另有五行字母串；密码学家尝试多种解法均告失败，甚至有人猜测与冷战间谍有关。至今这行密码仍静静躺在档案中。

**English**: The scrap in the dead man's pocket read "Tamam Shud" — Persian for "it is ended." Tracing it led police to the original book, which contained a phone number and, on its blank endpapers, a cipher: five letter groups with five more lines below. Cryptologists have tried every approach without success, and theories drift toward Cold War espionage. The cipher still sits unsolved in the case file.
