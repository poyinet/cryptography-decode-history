# 量子钞票备忘（Quantum Money Memo）

> 密件册 · 约 1970 · 美国 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`binary`

## 说明

**中文**：斯蒂芬·威斯纳《共轭编码》手稿的核心构想：用无法被克隆的量子态印制钞票，伪币制造者一旦测量就会破坏原态而暴露。稿件尘封十余年，却孕育了 BB84 与整个量子密码学。以下为构想要点的史料化节选。

**English**: The core idea of Stephen Wiesner's "Conjugate Coding" memo: print banknotes with quantum states that cannot be cloned — any counterfeiter who measures them disturbs the original state and gives himself away. The memo slept for a decade yet seeded BB84 and all of quantum cryptography. Below, a dramatized excerpt of its key idea.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：备忘录要点（史料化节选）：一、钞票序列号之外，嵌入若干二能级量子体系（如光子偏振态）；二、银行掌握发射基序列，可反复验证真伪；三、任何伪造者无法克隆未知量子态——测量必扰动，扰动即暴露；四、该性质源自「不可克隆定理」，是量子力学的推论而非工程承诺。

**English**: Memo highlights (dramatized excerpt): 1) Beyond serial numbers, embed two-level quantum systems (e.g., photon polarizations) in each note. 2) The issuing bank alone knows the basis sequence and can re-verify authenticity at will. 3) A counterfeiter cannot clone unknown quantum states — measurement disturbs, disturbance betrays. 4) This follows from the no-cloning theorem: a corollary of physics, not an engineering promise.
