# 肯迪手稿页（Al-Kindi's Manuscript）

> 密件册 · 9 世纪 · 性质：据史料复原 / Reconstructed · 关联游戏：`freq`

## 说明

**中文**：肯迪《解译加密信息手稿》残页，约 850 年写于巴格达智慧宫，首倡频率分析，是密码分析学的起点。

**English**: A page of Al-Kindi's Manuscript on Deciphering Cryptographic Messages (c. 850, Baghdad) — the first frequency analysis, the birth of cryptanalysis.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：（史料化引文节选）「破解密文之道：先统计密文中各字母出现的次数，再与该语言中字母的常用频率相对照；密文中出现最多的字母，很可能就是该语言最常用的字母。」

**English**: "One way to solve an encrypted message, if we know its language, is to count the letters: take the cipher text, list how often each letter occurs, and match the most frequent cipher letters to the most frequent letters of the language. For when a letter appears in the cipher more often than others, it is very likely the same letter that appears most often in the plain tongue."
(Adapted from Al-Kindi, A Manuscript on Deciphering Cryptographic Messages, c. 850.)
