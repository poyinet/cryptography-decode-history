# 多拉贝拉密信（Dorabella Cipher）

> 密件册 · 1897 年 · 英国伍斯特郡 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`substitution`

## 说明

**中文**：1897 年英国作曲家爱德华·埃尔加写给友人多拉·彭尼的密信，信上不是音符而是 87 个弯曲怪异的自创符号，被后世称为「多拉贝拉密码」；埃尔加本人热衷密码游戏，却始终未留密钥，百余年来破译尝试无数，至今无人能解。

**English**: In 1897 the English composer Edward Elgar sent his friend Dora Penny a note written not in music but in 87 strange, curling symbols of his own devising — the "Dorabella Cipher." Elgar loved cipher games yet left no key, and after more than a century of attempts it remains unsolved.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：密信原文共 87 个符号，分三行排列，仅 24 个符号各不相同，形如带尾巴的圆环与弧线，推测可能对应 24 个拉丁字母。埃尔加在信封上只写「多拉贝拉」（意为「美丽的多拉」）作为署名。有人猜是婚外情表白，有人说是音乐主题的变体，也有人主张只是给多拉的戏谑玩笑——但没有任何解读得到公认。

**English**: The note holds 87 glyphs arranged in three lines, yet only 24 distinct shapes — curling rings and arcs with trailing tails — suggesting a one-to-one mapping onto the 24-letter Latin alphabet. On the envelope Elgar wrote only "Dorabella" ("beautiful Dora"). Theories range from a declaration of love to a musical theme in disguise to a mere prank, but no reading has ever won acceptance.
