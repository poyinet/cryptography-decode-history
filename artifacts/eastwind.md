# 东风雨电报（The East Wind Broadcast）

> 密件册 · 1941 · 性质：依据史实的剧化 / Dramatized · 关联游戏：`purple`

## 说明

**中文**：日本外务省预定的开战暗号广播：若与美国开战，将播出「东风，有雨」（Higashi no kaze ame）。1941 年 12 月初，美军已破译紫密、截获外务省致华盛顿使馆的十四段电报——开战信号其实早已握在破译员手中，却在官僚链条里被延误与误判。

**English**: The planned Japanese "war signal" broadcast: should war with the United States begin, the weather code "East wind, rain" (Higashi no kaze ame) would be announced. In early December 1941 the US had already broken Purple and intercepted the Foreign Ministry's fourteen-part message to its Washington embassy — the signal was already in the codebreakers' hands, yet it was delayed and misjudged along the chain of command.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：〔原文节选 · 1941 年 12 月〕

◇ 预定暗号（日本外务省气象广播预案）：
「东风，晴＝与美国关系正常；东风，雨＝日美开战。」

◇ 截获的十四段电报（最后一段，节选）：
「……谈判已无意义，我方不得不作最后决定……」

——情报从不缺席，缺席的是相信它的人。

**English**: [Original excerpts · December 1941]

◇ The pre-arranged signal (Japanese Foreign Ministry weather-broadcast plan):
"East wind, clear — relations with the US normal. East wind, rain — war with the US."

◇ From the intercepted fourteen-part message (the final part, excerpt):
"...further negotiation is futile; His Majesty's Government must make its final decision..."

— The intelligence was never absent; what was absent was belief in it.
