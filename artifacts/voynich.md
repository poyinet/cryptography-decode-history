# 伏尼契手稿（Voynich Manuscript）

> 密件册 · 约 15 世纪初 · 中欧 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`wordsearch`

## 说明

**中文**：一部约 240 页的中世纪手稿，以无人能识的未知文字写就，满布奇异的天文、植物与沐浴插图，碳定年约属十五世纪初，五百余年无人能解，堪称密码史上最著名的未解之书。

**English**: A ~240-page medieval manuscript written in an unknown, undeciphered script, filled with strange astronomical, botanical and bathing illustrations; carbon-dated to the early 15th century, it has defied every attempt at decryption for over 500 years.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：全书约 240 页羊皮纸上写满未知字母，二十余种符号反复出现，却构不成任何已知语言；旋转的星图、无名的植物与沐浴的少女穿插其间，宛如异星密码。语言学家、密码学家乃至人工智能轮番上阵，至今一无所获——它没有语言、没有密钥，也没有作者。

**English**: About 240 pages of vellum covered in an unknown script — roughly two dozen recurring glyphs matching no known language. Rotating star charts, unidentifiable plants and bathing nymphs accompany the text, yet linguists, cryptologists and even AI have found nothing: no language, no cipher, no author.
