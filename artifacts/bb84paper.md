# BB84 会议摘要（The BB84 Abstract）

> 密件册 · 1984 · 印度班加罗尔 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`bb84`

## 说明

**中文**：贝内特与布拉萨德在 IEEE 国际会议系统科学分会发表的页摘要：首次给出实用的量子密钥分发协议——以光子偏振为骰子、以测不准原理为锁，窃听必然留下扰动指纹。量子密码学就此诞生。

**English**: Bennett and Brassard's one-page abstract at an IEEE conference session in Bangalore: the first practical quantum key distribution protocol — photons as dice, the uncertainty principle as the lock, eavesdropping betrayed by its own disturbance. Quantum cryptography was born here.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：摘要要点：A 与 B 借助一条量子信道与一条公开经典信道协商密钥；A 以随机选择的两组共轭基发送单光子，B 随机选基接收；双方公开比对基、保留匹配位；再抽样比对其中一部分以检测窃听——误码率异常即弃用本次密钥。安全性由量子力学保证，与算力无关。

**English**: Abstract points: Alice and Bob negotiate a key over one quantum channel and one public classical channel. Alice sends single photons encoded in randomly chosen conjugate bases; Bob measures in random bases. Both publish their bases and keep matching positions, then sacrifice a sample of bits: abnormal errors reveal eavesdropping and the key is discarded. Security rests on quantum mechanics itself — independent of any attacker's computing power.
