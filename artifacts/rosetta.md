# 罗塞塔石碑（The Rosetta Stone）

> 密件册 · 公元前 196 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`freq`

## 说明

**中文**：罗塞塔石碑，1799 年出土于埃及，同文三写：象形文、世俗体、希腊文，是破译古埃及文字的钥匙。

**English**: The Rosetta Stone, found at Rosetta, Egypt in 1799 — one decree carved in hieroglyphs, demotic and Greek; the key to ancient Egypt.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：（孟菲斯祭司诏令 · 史料化译文节选）「愿年轻的国王托勒密，永生者，普塔神所爱之人，万寿无疆。祭司们齐聚孟菲斯，立此石碑，以圣书体、世俗体与希腊文三体铭刻，使天下皆知其恩德。」

**English**: (Adapted translation — Decree of Memphis, 196 BC)
"In the reign of the young king Ptolemy, ever-living, beloved of Ptah, the priests gathered at Memphis and decreed: let images of the king be set up in every great temple, and let this decree be carved upon a stele of hard stone — in sacred writing, in the writing of documents, and in the writing of the Greeks — so that all people may know the goodness of the king."
