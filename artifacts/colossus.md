# Colossus 照片卡（Colossus Photo Card）

> 密件册 · 1943-45 · 性质：据史料复原 / Reconstructed · 关联游戏：`lorenz`

## 说明

**中文**：Colossus 是世界第一台可编程电子计算机，由汤米·弗劳尔斯设计，1943 年底完成，服役于布莱切利园的「纽曼里」。它用一千五百余只电子管，以每秒五千字符的速度对 Tunny 密文做差分统计，在诺曼底登陆前把德军最高层的命令送上英军破译员的桌子。战后机器被拆毁，秘密守到 1970 年代才解密。

**English**: Colossus — the world's first programmable electronic computer — was designed by Tommy Flowers and completed in late 1943, serving in the Newmanry at Bletchley Park. With more than 1,500 thermionic valves it ran delta statistics on Tunny traffic at 5,000 characters per second, putting the Wehrmacht's highest-level orders on the codebreakers' desks before D-Day. After the war the machines were dismantled; the secret held until the 1970s.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：〔Colossus 说明卡 · 史料化节选〕

Colossus Mk I · 1943 年 12 月完成，1944 年 2 月投入实战；
电子管约 1,500 只；纸带输入每秒 5,000 字符；
程序由插线与开关设定——可编程的电子计算机；
战后全部拆毁，图纸焚毁；1970 年代解密，1990 年代由志愿者重建。

——它没有登上教科书，却登上了历史。

**English**: [Colossus fact card · excerpt]

Colossus Mk I: completed December 1943, operational February 1944.
Valves: c. 1,500. Tape input: 5,000 characters per second.
Program set by plugs and switches — a programmable electronic computer.
After the war: dismantled, drawings burned. Declassified in the 1970s; rebuilt by volunteers in the 1990s.

— It never made the textbooks; it made history instead.
