# 美国内战密码盘（Civil War Cipher Disk）

> 密件册 · 1861–1865 · 美国 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`caesar`

## 说明

**中文**：南北战争联邦军信号兵团使用的双盘式密码盘：两枚同心铜盘错位旋转即完成字母替换；南军多次破译其电文，迫使联邦不断更换密钥与用法。

**English**: The twin-disk device of the Union Signal Corps: two concentric brass rings rotated against each other to substitute letters; Confederate codebreakers repeatedly read its traffic, forcing constant changes in Union practice.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：两盘同心铜盘，外盘为正常字母，内盘乱序；转动至约定偏移后逐字替换。战争中南军多次破译其电文，联邦军被迫频繁更换密钥，密盘遂成联邦信号密码的象征。

**English**: Two concentric brass disks — a normal outer alphabet, a scrambled inner one — slid to an agreed shift for letter-by-letter substitution. Confederate codebreakers read its traffic more than once, so the Union kept changing keys; the disk became the emblem of federal signal ciphers.
