# 费斯托斯圆盘（Phaistos Disc）

> 密件册 · 约公元前 1700 年 · 克里特岛费斯托斯 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`wordsearch`

## 说明

**中文**：1908 年在克里特岛费斯托斯王宫出土的黏土圆盘，两面以螺旋排列 45 种印刷式符号，约属公元前 1700 年，为世界上最早「活字印刷」的实物证据之一；其语言、用途与内容至今没有公认解读，是考古学与密码学交织的著名未解之谜。

**English**: Unearthed in 1908 at the Minoan palace of Phaistos, Crete, this fired-clay disc bears 45 distinct stamped symbols arranged in spirals on both faces — possibly the world's earliest "movable type" — dated to around 1700 BC. No agreed reading of its language, purpose or message has ever been reached.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：圆盘直径约 16 厘米，双面各有螺旋状符号环带，共刻 241 个印记，由 45 种不同符号组成，如人形、鱼、鸟、船与植物；符号以模具逐枚压印而成，暗示存在成批「印刷」的文字传统。有学者主张其为祈祷文或占卜用具，也有人怀疑是赝品，迄今尚无定论。

**English**: The disc is about 16 cm across, its two faces covered in spiral bands bearing 241 impressions made from 45 distinct sign-types — running men, fish, birds, ships and plants. Each sign was pressed with a separate stamp, hinting at a tradition of mass "printing." Scholars have proposed prayers, board-game pieces or even a forgery; no verdict has been reached.
