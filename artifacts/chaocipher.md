# 混沌密码（Chaocipher）

> 密件册 · 1918 年发明 · 美国波士顿 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`maker`

## 说明

**中文**：美国发明家约翰·F·伯恩 1918 年发明的便携密码机，他宣称该密码「不可破译」，1920 年代向军方与国务院推销却被拒；2010 年伯恩家族解密算法后，其原理公开——至今未发现可行的普通破解法，被视为天才的密码孤本。

**English**: A portable cipher machine invented in 1918 by American John F. Byrne — James Joyce's close friend — who insisted it was unbreakable. Rejected by the military and State Department in the 1920s, its algorithm stayed secret until Byrne's family released it in 2010; no practical shortcut attack is known even now.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：混沌密码的独特之处在于「双混字母表」：每次加密后，两个字母表都会按固定规则自行重组，使同一明文字母在不同位置加密成不同密文，旧式频率分析因此失效。伯恩曾将用其加密的挑战文本寄给《科学美国人》悬赏破解，无人成功；2010 年公开算法时，爱好者依说明在纸上即可复现整机。

**English**: The machine's genius lies in two permuting alphabets: after every encipherment both alphabets re-arrange themselves by fixed rules, so the same plaintext letter becomes different ciphertext at different positions — defeating old frequency analysis. Byrne offered the cipher to Scientific American as a challenge and nobody broke it; when the algorithm went public in 2010, hobbyists rebuilt the whole machine on paper from the description.
