# 朗格朗格（Rongorongo）

> 密件册 · 约 18–19 世纪 · 复活节岛（拉帕努伊） · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`wordsearch`

## 说明

**中文**：复活节岛上的刻字木板，以「诵唱」之意得名，岛民称其为会说话的木头；现存约 25 件残片，符号呈人鸟兽等象形与几何形，可能是波利尼西亚唯一的本土文字，1860 年代岛民识读传统中断后至今无人能解。

**English**: Rongorongo — "the chants" — are inscribed wooden tablets from Easter Island, which the islanders called "talking wood." About 25 fragments survive, their glyphs pictographic and geometric forms of humans, birds and other motifs. Possibly the only indigenous script of Polynesia, it lost its last readers in the 1860s and remains undeciphered.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：木板上刻着成排的符号，最特别之处在于阅读方向：每行从下往上读，隔行倒转 180 度，形成「牛耕式」蜿蜒的阅读路径；符号有约 400 个变体。传说木板记载着创世歌谣与族谱，1864 年传教士的记载提到岛民尚能「唱」读，此后文字失传，解读终成绝响。

**English**: The tablets bear rows of glyphs read bottom-to-top, with every other line rotated 180° in a "boustrophedon" serpentine path; roughly 400 glyph variants are known. Legend says they record creation chants and genealogies, and a missionary in 1864 noted islanders could still "sing" them — then the tradition died and the script fell silent forever.
