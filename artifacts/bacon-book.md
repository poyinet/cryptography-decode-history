# 培根书页（Bacon's Page）

> 密件册 · 17 世纪 · 性质：据史料复原 / Reconstructed · 关联游戏：`bacon`

## 说明

**中文**：培根《学术的进展》第 6 卷的一页：表面是一段谈论科学方法的散文，实际上每个字母的字形里都藏着第二封信——两种字体，两个故事。

**English**: A page from Book VI of Bacon's De Augmentis Scientiarum: outwardly an essay on scientific method, secretly a second letter hidden in the very shape of the letters — two fonts, two stories.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：—— 选自《学术的进展》第 6 卷（1623）

「凭借这门技艺，人可以跨越任何距离，用只能呈现两种差异的物件，表达并传递内心的意图；如钟声、号角、火炬之类。于是同一封信里可以携带两份书写：一份显而易见，一份隐秘——隐秘的那份，就藏在字母本身的形态之中。」

**English**: — from De Augmentis Scientiarum, Book VI (1623)

"For by this art a way is opened, whereby a man may express and signify the intentions of his mind, at any distance of place, with objects whose capacity is able to receive a difference only of two kinds; as bells, trumpets, torches, and the like. And in one and the same letter there may be carried two several writings: the one obvious, the other secret — the secret being conveyed in the fashion of the letters themselves."
