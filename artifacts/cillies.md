# Enigma「Cillies」操作员惰性密钥（The Enigma "Cillies"）

> 密件册 · 1940 年代 · 英国布莱切利园 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`bombe`

## 说明

**中文**：德军 Enigma 操作员为图省事，常选择容易的转子设置与密钥，如 AAA、ABC 或女友名字。布莱切利园破译员戏称这些偷懒密码为「cillies」，并以此作为推测当日密钥的捷径，大幅加快破译速度。

**English**: German Enigma operators, seeking convenience, often chose easy rotor settings and keys such as AAA, ABC, or girlfriends' names. Bletchley Park cryptanalysts jokingly called these lazy keys "cillies" and used them as shortcuts to deduce the day's key, greatly speeding up decryption.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：德军规定每日更换 Enigma 密钥，但操作员习惯偷懒：把三个转子设为 AAA、ABC 之类的连续字母，或使用可预测的初始位置。布莱切利园把这些可猜的密钥称为「cillies」（对 silly 的戏称）。破译员利用这种人性的弱点排除大量组合，甚至先猜密钥再验证，使 Bombe 的搜索空间骤减，成为密码史上「人是最薄弱环节」的经典案例。

**English**: German regulations demanded daily Enigma key changes, but operators habitually cheated: setting the three rotors to AAA, ABC, or other predictable positions. Bletchley Park called these guessable keys "cillies" (a pun on "silly"). By exploiting this human weakness, codebreakers eliminated huge numbers of combinations — sometimes guessing the key first and verifying it later — shrinking the Bombe's search space dramatically. It remains a classic case of the human factor being the weakest link in cryptography.
