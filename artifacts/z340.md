# 黄道杀手 340 密文（Zodiac 340 Cipher）

> 密件册 · 1969 年 11 月 · 美国旧金山 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`detective`

## 说明

**中文**：1969 年 11 月 8 日黄道杀手寄给《旧金山纪事报》的 340 字符密文，51 年间令 FBI 与全球爱好者束手无策；2020 年 12 月，三人民间团队以交叉比对法破译，读出「我希望你们在抓捕我时玩得开心」等语句，成为近年最轰动的民间密码破解。

**English**: The 340-character cipher mailed to the San Francisco Chronicle on November 8, 1969, defied the FBI and hobbyists alike for 51 years. In December 2020 a three-person amateur team cracked it by cross-matching repeated patterns, reading "I hope you are having lots of fun in trying to catch me" — the decade's most sensational amateur codebreak.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：破解文本全文大意：「我希望你们在抓捕我时玩得开心。我不怕毒气室，因为它会把我更快送上天，因为我现在已经有足够多的奴隶为我劳作……」，句中提及「天堂」与「奴隶」，呼应杀手自比极乐主宰的幻想；其余段落仍含拼写错误与自创词，学界对破解结论仍有保留意见。

**English**: The plaintext runs roughly: "I hope you are having lots of fun in trying to catch me. I am not afraid of the gas chamber because it will send me to paradice all the sooner because I now have enough slaves to work for me..." — complete with misspellings like "paradice" and fantasies of slaves and paradise. Some researchers still dispute the solution.
