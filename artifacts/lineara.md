# 线形文字 A（Linear A）

> 密件册 · 约公元前 1800–1450 年 · 克里特岛与爱琴海 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`wordsearch`

## 说明

**中文**：米诺斯文明使用的书写系统，刻于泥板、印章与祭器上，约 1400 件遗存；其后续文字线形文字 B 已于 1952 年被破译，证明是希腊语，而线形文字 A 所记录的语言至今无法确定，被称为爱琴海考古最大的未解之谜之一。

**English**: The script of Minoan civilization, preserved on clay tablets, seals and votive objects in some 1,400 inscriptions. Its successor, Linear B, was deciphered in 1952 and proved to be Greek; Linear A's underlying language remains unidentified — one of the great open questions of Aegean archaeology.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：线形文字 A 符号多为线条化的象形，兼有表音与计数记号，出现于宫殿泥板与宗教祭器上，常与「祭献清单」关联；学者试以希腊语、吕基亚语乃至印欧语系以外的语言破译，皆未获共识。1952 年迈克尔·文特里斯破译线形文字 B 时，曾寄望借此反推 A，最终仍功亏一篑。

**English**: Linear A consists of simplified pictographs with syllabic and numeral signs, found on palace tablets and cult vessels, often reading like offering inventories. Attempts to match it to Greek, Luwian or any known family have won no consensus; even Michael Ventris, after deciphering Linear B in 1952, hoped to work backward to A — in vain.
