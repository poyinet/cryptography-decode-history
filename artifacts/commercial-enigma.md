# 商业恩尼格玛（D 型）（Commercial Enigma (Model D)）

> 密件册 · 1923–1927 · 德国柏林 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`enigma`

## 说明

**中文**：谢尔比乌斯推向市场的民用转子密码机，曾在伯尔尼万国邮政大会公开演示；它没有插线板、密钥空间有限，却是德军军用恩尼格玛的直系前身。

**English**: Scherbius's civilian rotor machine, publicly demonstrated at the Bern Postal Congress; without a plugboard its key space was limited, yet it was the direct ancestor of the military Enigma.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：1924 年谢尔比乌斯携恩尼格玛 D 型亮相伯尔尼邮政大会，向各国推销这台「无法破译」的机器；德军以它为蓝本加装插线板改出军用型，日后让布莱切利园吃尽苦头。

**English**: In 1924 Scherbius presented the Enigma D at the Bern Postal Congress, pitching an “unbreakable” machine to the world's postal services; the Reichswehr rebuilt that commercial design with a plugboard into the military Enigma that would torment Bletchley Park.
