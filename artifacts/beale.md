# 比尔密码（The Beale Papers）

> 密件册 · 19 世纪 20 年代 · 美国弗吉尼亚 · 性质：依据史实的剧化 / Dramatized · 关联游戏：`detective`

## 说明

**中文**：1820 年代弗吉尼亚的藏宝传奇：三份密文声称记录一批巨额金银珠宝的埋藏位置，百余年过去仅第二份被破译，其余两份与宝藏下落至今成谜，真实性亦备受争议。

**English**: A Virginia treasure legend: three cipher texts supposedly reveal the hiding place of a vast hoard of gold and silver. After over a century only the second cipher has been solved; the other two, and the treasure, remain lost — and the story's authenticity is fiercely disputed.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：藏宝者留下三份密文：第一份指明藏宝地点，第二份列出财宝内容，第三份记下继承者姓名。1885 年小册子公布后，唯第二份以《独立宣言》为密钥被破译，读出金、银与宝石的惊人数量；其余两份任密码学家穷尽手段，仍如天书。

**English**: Three cipher texts were left behind: the first locates the vault, the second itemizes the treasure, the third names the heirs. Published in 1885, only the second — keyed to the Declaration of Independence — has ever been read, revealing astonishing quantities of gold, silver and jewels. The other two remain as impenetrable as the day they were printed.
