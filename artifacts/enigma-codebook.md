# Enigma 密钥本（The Enigma Codebook）

> 密件册 · 1939 年 · 欧洲 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`enigma`

## 说明

**中文**：1939 年夏，法国情报机构获得德军 Enigma 密码机的密钥资料与操作说明，经波兰转交英国。这批文件让布莱切利园得以系统研究德军加密体制，成为盟军破译 Enigma、赢得大西洋战役的关键起点。

**English**: In the summer of 1939, French intelligence obtained German Enigma key materials and operating manuals, which were passed on to Britain via Poland. These documents let Bletchley Park study the German cipher system systematically, making them the crucial starting point for breaking Enigma and winning the Battle of the Atlantic.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：1939 年 7 月，在波兰即将沦陷之际，波兰密码局将 Enigma 复原机与破译成果移交给英法。法国情报员古斯塔夫·贝特朗随后把截获的德军密钥资料与密码本交给英国，其中包含真实密钥表与操作流程。布莱切利园正是借助这些资料，结合图灵的 Bombe 设计，从 1940 年起持续破译德军密电，最终改变了二战进程。

**English**: In July 1939, as Poland was about to fall, the Polish Cipher Bureau handed reconstructed Enigma machines and their breakthroughs to Britain and France. French intelligence officer Gustave Bertrand then delivered captured German key documents, including genuine key tables and procedures, to Britain. Using these materials and Turing's Bombe design, Bletchley Park decrypted German traffic from 1940 onward, changing the course of World War II.
