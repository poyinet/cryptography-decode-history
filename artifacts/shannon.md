# 香农论文页（Shannon's Paper）

> 密件册 · 1948 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`xor`

## 说明

**中文**：1948 年，香农在《贝尔系统技术杂志》发表《通信的数学理论》，奠定信息论与密码学数学化的基石。以下为该论文开篇的节选。

**English**: In 1948 Shannon published "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" in the Bell System Technical Journal, laying the mathematical foundation of information theory and modern cryptography. Below is the paper's opening, excerpted.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：「通信的根本问题，是在一点精确或近似地复现另一点所选出的消息。通常这些消息带有意义……但这些语义方面与工程问题无关。」（节选·1948 年论文开篇）

**English**: "The fundamental problem of communication is that of reproducing at one point either exactly or approximately a message selected at another point. Frequently the messages have meaning... These semantic aspects of communication are irrelevant to the engineering problem." (Excerpt from the opening of the 1948 paper)
