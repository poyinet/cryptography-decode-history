# 黄道十二宫杀手「我的名字是」13 字符密文（The Zodiac "My Name Is" 13-Character Cipher）

> 密件册 · 1969 年 · 美国旧金山湾区 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：`detective`

## 说明

**中文**：1969 年 11 月，自称「黄道十二宫」的连环杀手向《旧金山纪事报》寄出密文，宣称其中 13 个字符暗藏他的真实姓名。五十年间无数人尝试破解，至今未获公认答案，成为悬案中最著名的未解密码之一。

**English**: In November 1969, the self-styled "Zodiac" serial killer mailed a cipher to the San Francisco Chronicle, claiming 13 of its characters concealed his real name. Fifty years of attempts have produced no generally accepted solution, making it one of the most famous unsolved ciphers in history.

## 全文 / 摘录

**中文**：这封「我的名字是」信件中，13 个字符据称代表凶手的真名。业余与专业密码学家提出过数百种解法，从斯拉夫姓氏到精巧的拼图，但没有任何一种得到官方与公众的一致认可。即使 FBI 在 2020 年宣布破解 340 字符的 z340 密文时，也明确表示这 13 字符仍未解开，令其成为密码史上最令人着迷的悬案之一。

**English**: In the "My Name Is" letter, 13 characters allegedly spelled the killer's true identity. Amateur and professional cryptanalysts have proposed hundreds of solutions, from Slavic surnames to elaborate puzzles, but none has won official or public acceptance. Even when the FBI announced the 2020 solution of the 340-character z340 cipher, it stated that these 13 characters remain unbroken — keeping them among cryptography's most intriguing mysteries.
